# Parody sheet

Every item and part mocks a staple of the genre: a power-up, an enemy, a gadget, a level cliché. This sheet is the brief for the art. Names come from the word lists in `src/gen/props.ts`, `src/sim/items/roster.ts` and `src/gen/setpieces/*`; the codex (`src/codex/`) uses the same names.

- **Ours only.** Our own names and art. Mock the trope first; a nod at one game gets a sound-alike or spoof name, never the real one, and never its characters, sprites or level layouts.
- **Never explain the joke.** No captions or labels that say what's being mocked. The silhouette shows the original and the gag gives the twist.
- **Fair first.** The gag lives in the art, inside the hitbox rules in [ART.md](ART.md#world-art-ink). A twist never hides a hazard's edge or its tell.
- **Objects, not characters.** No item, part, projectile or set piece has a face or a persona: no eyes, mouths, brows, blush or expressions, no little feet or hands that make it a creature, no hat or cap worn like a character. Only the runners have faces. The gag comes from the object's shape, material and mechanism, and a tell that once used eyes uses a mechanism instead: a sensor light, a creak, grit falling.

Each row gives what the item or part mocks, its silhouette (what must read at gameplay scale) and one visual gag.
