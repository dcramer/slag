# Parts art QA

A ranking of every part, launcher style and moving set piece against the bar: the main menu (`docs/ART.md`) and the best parts already in the game. Judged at play scale (`pnpm shot race --race all` on farm, factory and space) and close up (`pnpm shot parts`, `cards`, `buildparts`, `looks`, `pipes`, `parody`). Re-rank after any art pass; the bottom of this list is the art queue.

**The reference set:** the Great Wheel, the warp pipes, the girder and raft blocks, the loot box and the sentry. Each has a silhouette you can name from across the level, two or three hand-cut tones with a hard shadow, marks that say what it's made of, a mount you can see, and a telegraph that's a mechanism, not a glow.

## Ranking

Best first. "Redrawn" marks the parts this pass brought up from the bottom; their line says what they are now.

### At the bar

1. **Great Wheel** (set piece): spokes, cars and hub read at any size; the standard for a big mover.
2. **Warp pipes**: rolled lip that matches its solids, banded shaft, job tag; material variants all read.
3. **Girder / raft / ledge bridges**: bolted end plates, lashings, chiselled caps; marks sparse and keyed right.
4. **Loot box** (mystery): bevelled cube, own glyph, coin slot; squash on the punch is the best tell in the game.
5. **Sentry**: tripod, ammo box, twin barrels, a lamp for a tell; machine through and through.
6. **Pirate Cannon**: stepped carriage, trucks, map on the barrel; a silhouette with history.
7. **Dropstone** (Thwunk): carved bevels, stone teeth, grit when it shudders; the reference telegraph.
8. **Gumball machine** (Dodgeball Coach): globe with real glass glint, cast stand, whistle; reads instantly.
9. **Shuriken Vendor**: lit header, window of stars, coin slot, tray; best of the two-tile machines after the cannon.
10. **Ballista** on its tower: merlons, coursing, price tag; the bow string pulls on reload.
11. **Heartburn Idol** (redrawn): a carved tiki-torch post now: four turned drums (chevrons, a chili in relief, painted diamonds, flutes) on a flared plinth, a hammered bronze brazier with ring handles; the grooves glow and the fire climbs as it reloads, the next chili cooking in the flame.
12. **Kegapult / keg**: staves, hoops, aim chevrons; heat on the fuse is a good tell.
13. **Panini Press / crusher**: grill-marked head, guide rails; the Whack-a-Player's small head is thinner.
14. **Fuse Crate**: slats, stencil, the 3-2-1 paint-over; reads as a crate first, bomb second.
15. **Y-Fling**: forked stick, band, pouch; the band thinning under pull is the gag done right.
16. **Four-Square**: bevelled squares, one colour per piece; clean but pure geometry.
17. **Bumper**: skirt, rubber ring, star cap; the 100 pop helps it.
18. **Hangar** (Budget Airline): quonset, apron, windsock; paper planes are charming.
19. **Spike Block**: plates and horns read; the boot icon is lost at play scale.
20. **Saw / spin bar**: the blade's teeth and speed strokes read; Pizza Cutter handle is a good gag.

### Set pieces and vehicles

- **Forklift** (redrawn): the driver in a hard hat is gone (a person is a character: `docs/ART.md`, Objects, not characters); an empty bucket seat and a steering wheel on its column instead, a cast grille and bolts on the counterweight, rivets and a glint on the body.
- **Tractor** (redrawn): the farmer with a face and a straw hat is gone for the same reason; empty seat and wheel in the cab.
- **Minecart** (touched up): riveted iron straps round both ends and a dent; ore and the level sign were already good.
- **Ram, boulder, tunnel drill**: untouched; the ram's knocker ring and the boulder's film scratch carry them.
- **Geyser vent** (redrawn): the 9 px rock mound runners walked straight through is now a fissure flush with the ground: a spatter rim under 3 px (like the wall mouth), a wide dark crater sized to the column, cracks running off across the surface both ways (lit for lava), rubble and crust on the lips. Wider and fair, but low; a taller vent needs a sim solid (below).
- **Tracking camera** (fixed): its stalk now turns with a two-tile launcher, so on a wall it grows out of the machine instead of standing up into the air.

## Needs a sim change

- **Geyser vent mound.** To stand the vent up as a real cone (what "tiny" really asks for), the vent needs a solid: a stepped rim the sim stands on, in `src/sim/props/modules/geyser.ts`, and the column's `VENT_SKIP` raised to its lip. Until then the art stays under 3 px so it doesn't promise footing.
