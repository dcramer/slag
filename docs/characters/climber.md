- **Silhouette:** a small girl side on, a big messy mop of red hair floating up and back as if in the wind, a puffy parka to the hips, a small backpack, slim legs and boots; leaning into a climb or a dash.
- **Traits:** the big floating red hair; the puffy parka; the small backpack; small and determined; the dash (her hair streams back).

- **The nod:** the floating red hair (the read); the parka; her size.
