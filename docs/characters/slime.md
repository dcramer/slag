# The blue teardrop slime with the grin

A kind of the cast: [CHARACTERS.md](../CHARACTERS.md) has the rules every kind follows.

- **Kind:** `slime` · **Name:** Slimple
- **A stranger should say:** "the little blue slime from the old dragon RPG, the teardrop with the big smile."
- **Silhouette:** a small teardrop of goo, wide and flat at the bottom and rising to a pointed tip that curls over to one side, no arms or legs; two big round eyes high on it and a huge open grin across its middle. Moves by squashing flat and springing.
- **Traits:** the teardrop shape with the curled tip; the huge open grin; the big round eyes; the glossy shine; no limbs.
- **Palette:** the goo in the player's colour, its shine a light of it; the eyes and the mouth neutral (white and dark), the tongue a warm fixed tone (a face mark).
- **Color slot:** costume: the goo; the shine a light of it.
- **Gait:** limbless: every step a squash and a spring, a bigger squash on landing; it jiggles when it stops.
- **The nod:** the curled teardrop tip (the read); the grin; the limbless bounce.
- **Our twist:** it's a water balloon: the curled tip is the balloon's tied knot, a seam of the rubber shows, and a slow drip leaks from its back.
- **Swaps:** where the drip leaks from; a patch of tape over a pinhole; a knot left untied.
- **Rig:** drawn flat (2026-11-06, `art/sprites/slime/design.prompt.txt`), three-quarter facing right, about 21 px tall: two designs (both the original's onion, no balloon), two rounds of wear edits, and eleven pose drawings three to a 16:9 image, cut apart. The image model draws the onion whatever the twist says, so the balloon is the edit's: made the balloon's neck, the tip tied off a sack (a money bag in black); a big hooked curl read as a witch's hat ("witch or black mage, low-medium: tall cone hat with a tip that curls over", paired with the mage); the tip kept as drawn with a knot tied round its root, the design read named it ("Dragon Quest slime, high: an onion or teardrop body with a sharp curled point on top and a flat wide base"; "teardrop slime, medium-high"). Traced with `smooth: 0`, so the tip stays a point. The goo is the player's colour, the shine and the drop a light of it; the tape a neutral grey (its colourways greys: a brown parcel tape read as mud on the goo); the eyes the kit's `round`, the grin and its tongue as drawn. Every moving pose is a squash and a spring: its own springs drawn with two lobes under them read as legs, its lunge stretched long and low as a seal or a slug in black, and its leap stood half again over the box, so the run and the rise reuse the walk's spring. The blind sheet, two fresh readers: "Dragon Quest slime, high. Teardrop blob with a pointed top that flattens into a puddle when it runs"; "Dragon Quest slime, medium-high. Onion or teardrop shape with a point on top that flattens into a puddle when running". Neither paired it with another kind. Pairs to watch: the jelly (a dome: the slime's pointed tip must stand clear), the puffball (round with feet: the slime has none), the chomper (limbless and round: the tip and the grin carry it apart), the hopper, and the mage (a big hooked tip is a pointed hat).
- **Concept prompt:** A small cartoon blob of blue goo shaped like a teardrop, flat and wide at the bottom, rising to a pointed tip that curls to one side and is the tied knot of a water balloon, no arms or legs, two big round eyes high up, a huge open grin with a tongue, a glossy shine, a rubber seam and a slow drip leaking from its back, side on, cheerful.
