# The blue teardrop slime with the grin

A kind of the cast: [CHARACTERS.md](../CHARACTERS.md) has the rules every kind follows.

- **Kind:** `slime` · **Name:** Slimple
- **A stranger should say:** "the little blue slime from the old dragon RPG, the teardrop with the big smile."
- **Silhouette:** a small teardrop of goo, wide and flat at the bottom and rising to a pointed tip that curls over to one side, no arms or legs; two big round eyes high on it and a huge open grin across its middle. Moves by squashing flat and springing.
- **Traits:** the teardrop shape with the curled tip; the huge open grin; the big round eyes; the glossy shine; no limbs.
- **Palette:** the goo in the player's colour, its shine a light of it; the eyes and the mouth neutral (white and dark), the tongue a warm fixed tone (a face mark).
- **Color slot:** costume: the goo; the shine a light of it.
- **Gait:** limbless: every step a squash and a spring, a bigger squash on landing; it jiggles when it stops.
- **The nod:** the curled teardrop tip (the read); the grin; the limbless bounce.
- **Our twist:** it's a water balloon: the curled tip is the balloon's tied knot, a seam of the rubber shows, and a slow drip leaks from its back.
- **Swaps:** where the drip leaks from; a patch of tape over a pinhole; a knot left untied.
- **Rig:** to draw flat (2026-11-06). Pairs to watch: the jelly (a dome: the slime's pointed tip must stand clear), the puffball (round with feet: the slime has none), the chomper (limbless and round: the tip and the grin carry it apart), the hopper.
- **Concept prompt:** A small cartoon blob of blue goo shaped like a teardrop, flat and wide at the bottom, rising to a pointed tip that curls to one side and is the tied knot of a water balloon, no arms or legs, two big round eyes high up, a huge open grin with a tongue, a glossy shine, a rubber seam and a slow drip leaking from its back, side on, cheerful.
