# The mustached plumber

- **A stranger should say:** "that's the plumber with the mustache."
- **Silhouette:** stout and big-headed, about 45% head. A round cap with its bill sticking out front, a big round nose that breaks the profile, and a plunger as long as he is carried on his shoulder like a greatsword: the handle in his fist in front of his chest, over the shoulder, and the cup a bell of its own far out behind his head with background all round it, clear of the cap.
