# The bald hitman in the black suit and red tie

A kind of the cast: [CHARACTERS.md](../CHARACTERS.md) has the rules every kind follows.

- **Kind:** `hitman` · **Name:** Agent 46½
- **A stranger should say:** "the bald assassin with the barcode on the back of his head, in the black suit and red tie."
- **Silhouette:** tall, lean and upright, side on: a smooth bald head, a sharp dark suit with the jacket buttoned, a white shirt collar and a long tie down the front, gloved hands, a silenced pistol held low in each hand; a calm, still stance, never a crouch.
- **Traits:** the bald head; the barcode on the back of it; the black suit, white shirt and red tie; the leather gloves; the two pistols held low; the stillness.
- **Palette:** the suit in the player's colour (charcoal by default), its lapels a dark of it; the tie a light of it, far enough apart to stay its own shape; the shirt a white neutral; skin, gloves and pistols neutral; the barcode ink.
- **Color slot:** costume: the suit; the tie a light of it.
- **Gait:** an unhurried, even walk, arms still at his sides; his run stays upright; his cheer straightens the tie.
- **The nod:** the bald head with the barcode (the read); the suit with the shirt and tie; the twin pistols held low.
- **Our twist:** a bargain-bin knock-off: the barcode is a supermarket price sticker slapped on the back of his head, half peeling off, the tie is a clip-on hanging crooked, and the two silenced pistols are a pair of staplers.
- **Swaps:** where the sticker sits; the clip-on slipping; a staple sticking out.
- **Rig:** to draw flat (2026-11-07). Pairs to watch: the raider (twin pistols: hers are held out on a slim woman with a braid), the sneak (the other stealth kind), the warrior and the boxer (bald or round heads), the engineer.
- **Concept prompt:** A tall lean cartoon assassin standing side on, a smooth bald head with a supermarket price sticker barcode half peeling off the back of it, a sharp charcoal suit buttoned up, a white shirt collar and a crooked clip-on tie, black leather gloves, holding a stapler low in each hand like a silenced pistol, calm and expressionless.
