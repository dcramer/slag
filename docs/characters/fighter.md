# The bun-haired street fighter

A kind of the cast: [CHARACTERS.md](../CHARACTERS.md) has the rules every kind follows.

- **Kind:** `fighter` · **Name:** Bun-Li
- **A stranger should say:** "the street fighter with the two hair buns."
- **Silhouette:** always in a deep, wide fighting stance (or a kick), knees bent far apart over thick, strong thighs; huge round puffed shoulders wider than her small head; a short high-collared dress, its skirt flaring out over the thighs; two round hair buns up and out on the top corners of her head, her round crown showing between them; spiked bracelets on both wrists.
- **Traits:** the wide fighting stance; a high-collared qipao with huge puffed shoulders, a flared skirt and a sash; two hair buns in covers; spiked bracelets; thick muscular legs; white boots; a kick for a jump, a high kick for a cheer.
- **Palette:** the qipao in the player's colour, its trim a light of it; skin, hair, bun covers, bracelets, boots neutral.
- **Color slot:** costume.
- **Gait:** a bouncing fighter's stance on the spot; she runs low and fast, jumps into a flying kick and cheers with a high kick, one boot straight up beside her head.
- **The nod:** her pose and outfit first, the buns second: the deep, wide stance and the kicks (a fighting game's fighter: nobody else in the cast stands like it); the puffed shoulders and flared skirt over strong thighs (hers, never a gi); the two buns up and out, clear of her crown (her, never the headbanded fighter; round buns on a big round head are a bear's ears).
- **Our twist:** the bun covers are two rolled-up socks, the spiked bracelets are dog collars, and the sash is a dressing-gown cord.
- **Swaps:** a plaster on the knee; a sweatband; a ribbon come loose.
