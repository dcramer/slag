# The giant vampire lady in the big hat

A kind of the cast: [CHARACTERS.md](../CHARACTERS.md) has the rules every kind follows.

- **Kind:** `vampire` · **Name:** Lady Dimistretch
- **A stranger should say:** "the giant vampire lady from the castle, in the big white dress and the huge hat."
- **Silhouette:** the tallest the shared box allows and slim: a wide flat-crowned sun hat over a small head, a long gown flaring to the floor, one hand raised with five long claws, a string of pearls. Tall and straight, never round.
- **Traits:** the wide flat-crowned hat; the long gown to the floor; the long claw nails; pearls; pale skin and red lips; looking down at everyone.
- **Palette:** the gown in the player's colour; the hat a dark neutral, the skin pale, the lips red (a face mark), the pearls and claws neutral.
- **Color slot:** costume: the gown.
- **Gait:** a slow, grand glide, the gown hiding her feet; she has to duck to jump.
- **The nod:** the height under the wide hat (the read: a tall one for once); the gown; the claws.
- **Our twist:** the hat's brim droops on one side where it was sat on, the gown's hem is torn (worn shape, never mud: Decisions 9), and the claws are false nails, one of them missing.
- **Swaps:** which nail is missing; a snapped heel; a pearl gone from the string.
- **Rig:** drawn flat (2026-11-05, `art/sprites/vampire/design.prompt.txt`), three-quarter view, 28 px tall with the hat (the tallest in the cast: the hunter is 25), the air clip at 0.84 (she ducks to jump) and the wall and knockout inside the shared box. Twelve images, 24 credits: two designs, two wear edits, eight pose sheets. The wear edit bent the brim down at the back and tore the hem; the missing nail and pearl didn't land and wouldn't show at play size. The brim shades her eyes in the design and most poses (and where a pose draws them they are thinner than the ink), so the near eye is set by hand under the brim (`eyes.at`), the face kit's `heavy` eye, half-lidded, looking down at everyone, the far one a sliver. The hat is a dark neutral with two dark colourways; a plaster on the crown is her swap. Design read, design-1 and design-2 black at 28 px beside six cast designs at their heights, two fresh readers: "the tall vampire lady from Resident Evil Village (Lady Dimitrescu) - medium - very tall, thin figure in a long floor-length gown, wide-brimmed hat, one hand raised with a clawed or posed gesture"; "the tall vampire lady from Resident Evil Village (medium). Very tall and slim, wide-brimmed hat, long floor-length gown, one hand raised to the side." The blind sheet, two fresh readers on 4: "giant vampire lady (Lady Dimitrescu), medium, very tall and thin, wide-brimmed hat, long flowing dress"; "Lady Dimitrescu, the tall vampire lady from Resident Evil Village (medium). Very tall and thin, wide-brim hat, long flowing dress flaring at the hem." Neither paired her with another kind; passed on the first sheet. Her height carried it: the mage's brim sits on a small round figure, hers on the tallest one.
- **Concept prompt:** A very tall slim cartoon lady in three-quarter view, a huge wide black sun hat with a flat crown, its brim drooping on one side, over a small pale face with red lips, a long elegant gown to the floor with a torn hem, a string of pearls, one hand raised with five very long claw-like false nails, one nail missing, looking down haughtily.
