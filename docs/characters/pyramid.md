# The triangle-helmeted executioner

A kind of the cast: [CHARACTERS.md](../CHARACTERS.md) has the rules every kind follows.

- **Kind:** `pyramid` · **Name:** Pyramid Fred
- **A stranger should say:** "the hulking executioner with the huge triangle helmet and the giant blade."
- **Silhouette:** a hulking figure, the head one tall upright rusty pyramid sitting straight down on his broad square shoulders, its point straight up, taller than his torso, its base flat and as wide as his shoulders, its edges hard and straight and its two faces flat, one lit and one shaded, meeting at a ridge (no neck, no brim, nothing sticking out sideways; rounded or one flat colour, it is a hood); an apron to the knees over two thick legs apart; bare thick arms; a long flat blade dragged behind him, a steep diagonal from his fist to the floor.
- **Traits:** a huge triangle helmet for a head, its point straight up; an apron; thick arms; a giant flat blade dragged behind; a slow, heavy walk.
- **Palette:** the apron in the player's colour, the pig printed on it a light of it; the helmet a neutral rust (or an old brown rust, or a weathered iron), its far face a shade of it, the skin a pale grey, the blade steel, the boots dark brown.
- **Color slot:** costume: the apron.
- **Gait:** a slow, heavy drag, the blade scraping behind; never runs, only lurches.
- **The nod:** the triangle helmet (the read: a huge triangle for a head on a hulk, standing straight on his shoulders; overhanging them, out sideways or with a wide base, it is a brimmed hat); the giant blade dragged behind (the second read, which turns a cone on a body into the executioner; low and behind, never across his legs, where it flares his base into a robe).
- **Our twist:** the helmet is a rusty roof-vent cowl, the blade an enormous cheese slicer, and the smock a butcher's apron with a cartoon pig on it.
- **Swaps:** a sticking plaster on the helmet's face. The torn hem and the bitten slicer are his wear, on every look (the drawn rivets and dent went with the drawn cowl: as specks along a headless helmet's base they would read as eyes).
