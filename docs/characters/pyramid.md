# The triangle-helmeted executioner

A kind of the cast: [CHARACTERS.md](../CHARACTERS.md) has the rules every kind follows.

- **Kind:** `pyramid` · **Name:** Pyramid Ted
- **A stranger should say:** "the hulking executioner with the huge triangle helmet and the giant blade."
- **Silhouette:** a hulking figure, the head a huge rusty wedge of a helmet, a right-angled triangle wider than his shoulders, its point sticking out past his front shoulder with background above and below it; an apron to the knees over two thick legs apart; bare thick arms; a huge flat blade dragged behind him, a long diagonal from his fist to the floor.
- **Traits:** a huge triangle helmet with its point out to one side; an apron; thick arms; a giant flat blade dragged behind; a slow, heavy walk.
- **Palette:** the apron in the player's colour, the pig printed on it a light of it; the helmet a neutral rust (or galvanised grey, or sooty iron), the skin a pale grey, the blade steel, the boots dark brown.
- **Color slot:** costume: the apron.
- **Gait:** a slow, heavy drag, the blade scraping behind; never runs, only lurches.
- **The nod:** the triangle helmet (the read: a huge triangle for a head, its point out sideways, never up like a hat); the giant blade dragged behind (low and behind, never across his legs, where it flares his base into a robe).
- **Our twist:** the helmet is a rusty roof-vent cowl, the blade an enormous cheese slicer, and the smock a butcher's apron with a cartoon pig on it.
- **Swaps:** a sticking plaster on the helmet's crown. The dent, the rivets, the torn hem and the bitten slicer are his wear, on every look.
