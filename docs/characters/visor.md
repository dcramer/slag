# The impostor crewmate

- **Kind:** `visor` · **Name:** Sussy
- **A stranger should say:** "the crewmate from the impostor game."
- **Silhouette:** a bean on two stubby legs, a wide visor standing proud of the front, and a pack behind.
- **Traits:** a smooth bean body; a wide pale visor; a backpack; no arms.
- **Palette:** costume (the player's): the body, because the original comes in every colour; the can a dark of it (tone −16). Visor a neutral glass, pale cool grey `#c4ccd2` with a darker rim `#7c8893` (in the original's pale blue `#a9dbe8` it nearly vanished on cyan and light-blue beans); tape `#eceeef`, lighter than the glass (silver duct tape: in the strap's pale tan it was the farm's own colour, and read as a notch cut in the visor; in brown packing tape, a brow); strap `#d9c79a`; the sticky note `#f4e38a` (owner).
- **Gait:** a waddling shuffle on its stubby legs.
- **The nod:** the bean on stubby legs (the whole silhouette); the wide pale visor (the face; without it it's a jelly bean); the pack on its back (it breaks the outline behind, and is half the bean's read in profile).
- **Our twist:** a crew issued from the scrapyard. The pack is a jerrycan with a spout, strapped on; a crack across the visor is taped over; a dent in its side has been knocked out with a hammer.
- **Swaps:** a sprout growing from the head; a sticky note on the visor; a paper sailor hat; a bandage round one leg.
- **Palette alts:** the strap in tan webbing `#d9c79a`, grey webbing `#b4b9bf` or bone `#ece4d2`.
- **Rig:** drawn flat, side on: a tape strip across the visor's crack, a hammered dent in its side, a crumpled can and a bent spout; ten poses, and a second idle cut from the design rocked back on its heel (the image model held the bean to the design's stance). No face kit: the visor is drawn as it is. First drawn with the can as tall as the bean, fused with its back, and the visor inside the outline: "a squarish rounded blob with small nubs", "a boxy, heavy blob" (unsure twice); so the design's visor was moved out past the front and the can back off the bean onto a short strap, by hand, and the poses copied both: high twice ("bean body, visor bump out front, backpack slab on the back"). The can's handle hole is cut through and its spout's white loop folded into the can: white like the visor's glint, they read as an eye on the pack. The visor is blank glass, the crack painted out of the drawings and the glint folded in: the crack's zigzag read as a frown, and the small white glint, inked round, as an eye.
- **Concept prompt:** A smooth bean-shaped spacesuit body on two stubby legs, no arms; a wide pale-blue visor with a white glint standing proud of its front, a crack across it taped over; a red jerrycan with a yellow-capped spout strapped to its back as a backpack; a hammered-out dent in its side.
