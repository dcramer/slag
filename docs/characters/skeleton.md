# The punny skeleton in the hoodie

A kind of the cast: [CHARACTERS.md](../CHARACTERS.md) has the rules every kind follows.

- **Kind:** `skeleton` · **Name:** Humerus
- **A stranger should say:** "the lazy skeleton from the indie RPG, in the blue hoodie with the big grin."
- **Silhouette:** short and stocky: a big round white skull with a fixed wide grin, two dark eye sockets (one with a pale glowing pupil), a baggy hoodie with a fur-trimmed hood down, hands in its pockets, baggy shorts, thin bone legs and slippers.
- **Traits:** the big round skull with the permanent wide grin; the dark eye sockets, one lit; the baggy hoodie with the fur hood; hands in the pockets; the shorts and slippers; a lazy slouch.
- **Palette:** the hoodie in the player's colour, the ketchup bottle a light of it; the skull and bones a bone-white neutral, the teeth whiter; the fur trim and the slippers one mid grey, apart from the skull; the shorts a dark neutral; the eye glow a cool fixed tone (a face mark).
- **Color slot:** costume: the hoodie.
- **Gait:** an easy, lazy shuffle, hands in pockets; his jump is a lazy hop with a wink; on a stop he leans.
- **The nod:** the round grinning skull (the read); the hoodie with its fur hood; the hands in the pockets.
- **Our twist:** he's a plastic Halloween decoration come loose: a moulding seam runs over the skull and rivets show at his joints, the hoodie is a hand-me-down with a ragged hem, and a ketchup bottle sticks out of the pocket. A hanging loop on the crown was drawn and cut: at play size it turned the round skull into a ghost's head, an onion, an egg or an antenna.
- **Swaps:** a plaster on the cranium; the fur and slippers in a grey, a darker grey or a fawn. The far socket is the lit one, in every look; the knee's missing rivet and the bottle's open cap are worn into the drawing.
- **Rig:** drawn flat (2026-11-06; art/sprites/skeleton/design.prompt.txt), the drawings made ready by prep.py: the sockets solid under the face kit's `socket` eye (the far one lit), the teeth one white band in a dark rim, a nose of its own, the crown redrawn round. Pairs to watch: the cuptoon (a big round white head: the skull's grin and sockets carry it apart), the limbless hero, the puffball (round and small), the boxer. Blind reads in colour: the drawings as cut, "unsure, skull-like head, pale green body" twice; the loop bigger, "ghost-like head" twice; the teeth and nose, "Siegward, the onion knight ... onion-shaped helmet" and "skull-like head"; the crown round with a small ring, "egg-shaped head" twice; no loop, "Sans (Undertale) — medium" twice; a ring on a neck, "a white round head with an antenna" and "skull-like head". Ships without the loop: "Sans (Undertale) — low — skull head, chunky jacket" and "Sans (Undertale). Medium: round skull head, baggy top", neither paired.
- **Concept prompt:** A short stocky cartoon skeleton standing side on, a big round white plastic skull with a fixed wide grin and a moulding seam, two dark eye sockets with one pale glowing pupil, a baggy hoodie two sizes too big with a fur-trimmed hood down, hands in the pockets with a ketchup bottle sticking out of one, baggy shorts, thin bone legs with rivets at the joints, slippers, a lazy slouch.
