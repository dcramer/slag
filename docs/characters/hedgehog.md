# The blue speedster

- **Kind:** `hedgehog` · **Name:** Zoomic
- **A stranger should say:** "the fast blue hedgehog."
- **Silhouette:** a big head with four or five long spikes swept straight back, a small body, thin legs, big boxy shoes, and a big wind-up key sticking straight out of his hip behind him, its figure-eight bow standing clear of the quills.
- **Traits:** cobalt quills swept back; green eyes; a peach muzzle and belly; a cocky one-sided grin; big red shoes.
- **Palette:** costume (the player's, `#2f5bd0` on the sheets): the tin quills, head and body; the quills' folded edge a light of it (tone +30), the tin shoes a dark of it (tone −22). Muzzle, belly and mitten hands `#f0c99c`; irises `#3a9a46`; the key, soles and shoe straps steel `#9a9d9f`.
- **Gait:** runs leaning hard forward, legs a blur; curls into a spinning ball in the air.
- **The nod:** the cobalt quills swept straight back (they are the whole silhouette); the big red shoes (the second thing anyone draws); the peach muzzle and belly (they tell a hedgehog from a blue blob); green eyes and the cocky grin (the attitude); the forward-leaning run and the spin-ball jump (the speedster in motion, readable when the shape blurs).
- **Our twist:** the fastest thing alive is an old pressed-tin wind-up toy, and he has to be wound up. A big wind-up key sticks out of his hip in every pose (it turns round his spin ball, and stands up out of him when he's run down flat); the quills are bent tin strips with a pale folded edge; head and body are tin halves on riveted seams; the shoes are boxy tin blocks on grey soles, the hands peach tin mittens. (The scrapyard runner before it, a chipped tooth, a hazard neckerchief and steel toecaps, shrank to specks at play size and read as the original redrawn: `art/sprites/hedgehog/scrapyard/`.)
- **Swaps:** a sticking plaster across the cheek.
- **Palette alts:** the key, soles and straps in steel `#9a9d9f`, bright tin `#bcbfc0` or dark iron `#76797c` (brass and copper keys sank into an orange or tan player's tin).
- **Idles and cheers:** the idle from the model; arms folded, tapping one toe. The model's cheer, and its later both-fists-up stretch.
- **Gait range:** a bounce of up to 0.45 px and a lean of 0.01–0.06 on top of the hard forward lean he already runs with.
- **Wear:** in the drawing, a dent in the top of his tin head, the lowest quill strip bent at a kink and the front shoe's sole sprung open; scuffed is a scuff on the belly, scorched a scorch there (two marks or a strong tone on the belly read as a rash).
