# The jellyfish parasite

- **Kind:** `jelly` · **Name:** Wobbletroid
- **A stranger should say:** "the floating jellyfish thing from the space game."
- **Silhouette:** a dome with four fangs hooked underneath: two big ones under its middle curving in to a pincer, a small one each side; it floats and never touches the floor.
- **Traits:** a see-through green membrane; three red nuclei inside it; a pale rim; four ivory fangs.
- **Palette:** costume (the player's, `#9ee38a` on the sheets): the dome; the cherries and the mint a dark of it, the rim a light of it. Fangs a bone `#efe6d2`.
- **Gait:** hovers with a slow bob and chomps its fangs, faster when moving.
- **The nod:** the translucent green dome (the only see-through body in the cast); the three red nuclei (they make it this parasite, not a jellyfish); the four fangs hooked underneath (the menace); floating (it never walks).
- **Our twist:** a lime jelly dessert that got ideas. The dome wobbles like set gelatine, the nuclei are three glacé cherries, the fangs are sugared almonds, and a paper doily is stuck to its underside.
- **Swaps:** a cocktail umbrella stuck in the top; a sprig of mint; a spoon-dent; a wafer fan.
- **Palette alts:** the sugared-almond fangs in ivory `#efe6d2`, old bone `#e0cfa8` or grey bone `#d6d4cb`.
- **Rig:** drawn flat, no face and no glass: the dome a flat colour, turned out of a fluted mould and flaring at its rim like a bell, worn with a melting drip down one side and a spoonful scooped out of its top. The fangs are the parasite's mandibles: two big ones, half the dome's height, hang from under the middle of the rim and curve in until their tips nearly cross, a pincer, with a small one each side just inside the rim. The fangs' spacing is the read. First drawn: low twice, "a dome with a fringed or legged bottom edge". Redrawn: one reader at medium; the other "an octopus/tentacle alien, medium… might be" it (its long hooks curl like tentacles). Short fangs evenly along the rim read as the dome-headed ghost's hem, short hooked fangs at the rim's edge as the parasite, the ghost or the ape's arms by a few pixels. The cherries are a dark of the dome with no rib lines (a rib down a cherry read as a crack); the doily isn't drawn (between the fangs it merged them into one pale skirt). Three drawings carry every clip (the mandibles hanging, pinched shut, spread): asked to move its fangs, the image model adds more, so the two poses are the design with its fangs swung about their roots. The chomp is spread and pinched on the walk and run; the swap is a sprig of mint on the dome.
- **Concept prompt:** A floating translucent lime-green jelly dome that wobbles like set gelatine, with three glacé cherries suspended inside it like nuclei, a pale rim, and four ivory sugared-almond fangs hooked underneath; a paper doily stuck to its underside. It hovers above the ground and never touches it.
