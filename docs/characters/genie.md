# The half-genie who whips her long ponytail

A kind of the cast: [CHARACTERS.md](../CHARACTERS.md) has the rules every kind follows.

- **Kind:** `genie` · **Name:** Shampootae
- **A stranger should say:** "the half-genie dancer from the platformer who attacks with her long purple ponytail."
- **Silhouette:** a chunky little dancer (slim, she read as a candle under a wisp), side on, arms out, a thick ponytail springing up high from the top of her head and falling back behind her like a fountain, with background between it and her head and back, a small tiara on her brow, a cropped top, two balloon harem trousers gathered at the ankle with background between the legs, big hoop earrings. The ponytail is the read: longer than any hair in the cast, and up from the crown, since the box has height to spare and not width.
- **Traits:** the ponytail as long as she is, from the crown; whipping it as an attack; the tiara; the puffy harem trousers; the hoop earrings; bare midriff.
- **Palette:** the top and the trousers in the player's colour (drawn `#cf236c`), the sash a dark of it (−30); the hair purple `#7c49a5` (a fixed tone the read needs, like the slayer's white), skin `#dea36b`, the ring and hoops white `#eceff6`, the foil and tape `#c4c8cc`.
- **Palette alts:** the foil tiara and the tape in silver `#c4c8cc`, gold foil `#d8bc6c` or a pale steel `#a8b0b8`.
- **Idles and cheers:** the idle is the dance, arms out; a hand on her hip, the other arm out. The hair whip, leaning in with her fists back and the ponytail cracked out level, eyes squeezed shut; hands on her hips with a big grin.
- **Gait range:** a bounce of 0.4–0.9 px on each step, a lean of up to 0.03.
- **Wear:** in the drawing, the near trouser hem snagged into a ragged strip, the tiara's point bent over, tape round the ponytail, the sash's end frayed; scuffed is a pale scuff on the trousers, scorched a soot mark there (tones of the costume).
- **Color slot:** costume: the top and the trousers; the sash a dark of it.
- **Gait:** a bouncy dancer's skip; her cheer is a hair whip, the ponytail cracking out level; her jump flicks the ponytail up behind.
- **The nod:** the ponytail whipped out level (the read); the tiara; the harem trousers.
- **Our twist:** her ponytail is held by a shower-curtain ring, the tiara is a tin-foil crown, the hoop earrings are curtain rings, and the harem trousers are net curtains, one hem snagged.
- **Swaps:** which ring is missing off the ponytail; a foil corner bent; a snag in the net.
- **Rig:** drawn flat (2026-11-06, `art/sprites/genie/design.prompt.txt`), side on, body 112 (the walk 0.93, the run 0.92, the air 0.8 and the wall 0.86 of it, to keep the fountain in the box): four designs in two rounds, a wear edit, a rope edit and a fountain edit, and thirty-six pose drawings three to a 16:9 image in three rounds, cut apart (`r1/` the plume, `r2/` the rope). The eye is a small dark oval narrower than the map's ink, so `prep.py` paints it apart first (the `eye` role, found in the map). Pairs to watch: the raider (a slim woman with a single braid behind: the genie's tail is twice as long and from the crown), the archer's bushy braids, the fighter (a dancer's stance, not a fighter's), the succubus. The readers of rounds 1 and 2 and of the first design reads were subagents whose context named the kinds; from round 3 every reader is `tools/art3d/blind-read.sh`. Design reads, the black design among cast silhouettes, blind: the plume "unsure — huge sweeping tail or ponytail curling up to the left, round puffy lower body like a skirt or balloon pants"; the fountain "a huge plume of hair or a crest swept up and over the head, with a wide skirt or dress below" twice; the fountain with background between the trouser legs "Shantae — medium — a very tall whip-like ponytail rising high above the head, and flared, puffy pants" and "Shantae — medium — very long ponytail whipping up and over, puffy harem-style pants, slim female figure". The blind sheet, three rounds of two fresh readers:
  - Round 1, the ponytail a big plume swept back from the crown, body 88: "Tails or a fox or squirrel (low). A big curled tail bigger than the body" and "maybe Tails or a squirrel like Conker, low, small body with a big curling tail or ponytail". The plume meets the back of her head, so it is a tail.
  - Round 2, a straight level rope from the ring, body 88: "a worm from Worms, low. Small figure aiming a long bazooka sideways" and "tiny figure with a huge bazooka, maybe a Worms worm or Metal Slug, low". A bar level at head height is a gun.
  - Round 3, the fountain, body 112: "unsure — curled hook or tail shape over a small body" and "unsure — tall curved hat or tail". At play size the pen closes the gap between the trouser legs that made the mock read, and the fountain spends her height, so her body is small beside the cast again.
  - Stopped after rounds 2 and 3, which only moved the misread. Every round called her small: the box gives a ponytail as long as she is room only at the body's expense, whichever way it goes. The same blind readers named few drawn kinds either (the raider, the archer, the succubus and the horned bug all "unsure").
