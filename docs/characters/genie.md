# The half-genie who whips her long ponytail

A kind of the cast: [CHARACTERS.md](../CHARACTERS.md) has the rules every kind follows.

- **Kind:** `genie` · **Name:** Shampootae
- **A stranger should say:** "the half-genie dancer from the platformer who attacks with her long purple ponytail."
- **Silhouette:** a chunky little dancer (slim, she read as a candle under a wisp), side on, arms out, a ponytail as long as she is springing from the top of her head and whipping out level behind or ahead of her, a small tiara on her brow, a cropped top, puffy harem trousers gathered at the ankle, big hoop earrings.
