# The white-haired monster slayer

A kind of the cast: [CHARACTERS.md](../CHARACTERS.md) has the rules every kind follows.

- **Kind:** `slayer` · **Name:** Gerald
- **A stranger should say:** "the white-haired monster hunter with two swords."
- **Silhouette:** broad-shouldered and thick-chested in a wide stance, a short coat to mid-thigh over two legs in tall boots, the white hair tied tight back in a short tail, a long straight silver sword in his hand held low, and two straight hilts side by side above his back shoulder, clear of the head. Never a long coat or loose hair: as a slim figure in a coat to the knees, a full-cast blind read took him for a princess in a gown.
- **Traits:** white hair tied back; two blades (one in hand, one on his back); a wolf's-head medallion on a chain; cat's eyes, yellow with slit pupils; a scar down over one eye; a leather coat, gloves and tall boots; a grim mouth.
- **Palette:** the coat in the player's colour (drawn a dull green `#5a6250`); the gloves, the boots, the shoelace and the umbrella's handle a dark of it (−26), as dyed leather (drawn in one brown, so they can't be told apart). The trousers and the sword's grip are drawn in the coat's shade, so they are the coat too. The rest neutral: hair `#f2f0ec`; skin `#f0cbb2`; the eyes' yellow `#e0c850` and the kit's iris `#e0b830`; steel (both swords, the bottle cap) `#b4bac2`; the soles and the umbrella's furled cloth `#3e3e44`.
- **Color slot:** costume: the coat, the gloves and boots a dark of it.
- **Palette alts:** the steel in bright steel `#b4bac2`, old iron `#8c8e92` or a brass-painted cap `#bca878`.
- **Gait:** a steady, wary prowl, the sword held low.
- **The nod:** the long straight sword in hand, held low in a guard (a swordsman's, where the elf carries a poker and the merc a bread knife bigger than he is); the two hilts over the shoulder (a second blade on his back); the broad, male build in a short coat (not a princess, not the slim elf); the white hair, the medallion and the cat's eyes (in colour).
- **Our twist:** the second sword on his back is a furled black umbrella, its wooden handle the second hilt, and the wolf medallion is a crimped bottle cap pressed with a wolf's head, on a shoelace.
- **Swaps:** a plaster on the scar (`plaster`, the cheek). The hay and the torn sleeve aren't drawn: inside the outline at play size each is a speck.
- **Idles and cheers:** the idle from the design, in a guard; a hand on the bottle cap. A fist punched forward with a grim grin; the sword resting on his shoulder with a smirk.
- **Gait range:** a bounce of 0.1–0.5 px on each step, a lean of up to 0.03.
- **Wear:** none in this drawing (the redraw's credits went to the outline); scuffed is two pale scuffs on the coat, scorched two soot marks there, both tones of the coat.
- **Rig:** drawn flat twice (2026-11-04, art/sprites/slayer/design.prompt.txt; the first version and its prompts in r1/). Both are three-quarter, 24 px tall. `whites.py` tints the white hair a step off white and folds its blue-grey shade into it, or `holes` cut the hair out as background and the shade traced as steel. The eyes are found by their yellow (a role of its own: the slit pupils are thinner than the ink), the near one with the far one set from it, and drawn by the face kit's `cat` eye, added to `EYES` for him: an almond all iris round a slit, so the yellow shows at any size.
  - **The first version**, tall and lean in a long coat with the hair in a ponytail, went through three blind rounds. The hilts one each side of the head read as horns ("Viking or barbarian warrior, maybe Geralt, low"; "Hollow Knight, low. Horns or antennae on top"). Slung over one shoulder, it read as the elf ("a white-haired monster slayer like Geralt, or Link, low ... possibly 14 and 26"). A later full-cast read (100 px cells) took it for "Princess Peach or Zelda, low. Slim, with long hair and a dress or gown silhouette".
  - **The redraw**, an edit of the first version's design: broad shoulders, a short coat open over two legs, the hair tight, a sword in hand. Asked three times to hold the blade clear of his legs, the image model kept it across them. Design reads moved the misread: thin hilts were "a hooded archer or ranger ... a quiver" (and paired with the elf); a crook was "a hooded robed wizard ... a staff with a curled hook"; two straight hilts and a straight coat were "monster hunter with swords on the back (Witcher/Geralt parody), low" and "a three-pronged shape (a trident, fork or plume)", unpaired. That version ships. On the blind sheet: "unsure. Long rabbit ears and a sword or stick (maybe Jazz Jackrabbit)" and "Jazz Jackrabbit, low. Very long upright ears, a slim body, a weapon or sword held out". Neither paired him with another kind. The two parallel hilts with round pommels read as ears. Crossed in an X (drafts/cross-*), the design read was "a sword hilt sticking up diagonally behind the left shoulder, a hooked staff or crook" and "adventurer with a sword on the back (low)", so the hilts weren't redrawn.
  - **Not passed.** The misread has moved six times (horns, the elf, a princess, a ranger, a wizard, a rabbit). In black at play size, a swordsman with a white head has no shape nobody else's has: his read is in colour (the white hair, the cat's eyes, the cap).
- **Concept prompt:** A broad-shouldered cartoon monster hunter in three-quarter view, standing in a wide stance, white hair pulled back tight into a short tail, a long straight silver sword held low in his hand, two straight hilts side by side above his back shoulder (a steel sword's cross-guard and a furled black umbrella's wooden handle), cat's eyes, yellow with slit pupils, a scar down over one eye, a crimped bottle cap pressed with a wolf's head on a shoelace, a short green leather coat to mid-thigh open at the front, dark trousers, gloves and tall brown boots.
