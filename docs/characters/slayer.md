# The white-haired monster slayer

A kind of the cast: [CHARACTERS.md](../CHARACTERS.md) has the rules every kind follows.

- **Kind:** `slayer` · **Name:** Gerald
- **A stranger should say:** "the white-haired monster hunter with two swords."
- **Silhouette:** tall and lean in a long coat flared to the knees, the white hair tied back in a ponytail down his back, and a sword and a furled umbrella slung together over one shoulder: their two handles stick up and back behind it, clear of the head, and their tips out below the coat at the other hip. Taller and leaner than the merc and the elf, never a hood.
- **Traits:** long white hair in a ponytail; two blades on his back; a wolf's-head medallion on a chain; cat's eyes, yellow with slit pupils; a scar down over one eye; a long leather coat, gloves and tall boots; a grim mouth.
- **Palette:** the coat in the player's colour (drawn a dull green `#5a6250`), the gloves and boots a dark of it (−26), as dyed leather; the sword's grip is drawn in the coat's shade, so it is the coat too. - **Color slot:** costume: the coat, the gloves and boots a dark of it.
- **Palette alts:** the steel in bright steel `#b4bac2`, old iron `#8c8e92` or a brass-painted cap `#bca878`.
- **Gait:** a steady, wary prowl.
- **The nod:** the white hair (the only white hair in the cast); the two handles over the shoulder (the outline: two things strapped on his back, where the elf's poker is in his hand); the long coat; the medallion and the cat's eyes (in colour).
- **Our twist:** the second sword is a furled black umbrella, its crook the second handle and a broken spoke poking out of its cloth, and the wolf medallion is a crimped bottle cap pressed with a wolf's head, on a shoelace.
- **Swaps:** a plaster on the scar (`plaster`, the cheek). The hay and the torn sleeve aren't drawn: inside the outline at play size each is a speck.
- **Idles and cheers:** the idle from the design; a hand on the bottle cap. A fist punched forward with a grim grin; arms folded with a smirk.
- **Gait range:** a bounce of 0.1–0.5 px on each step, a lean of up to 0.03.
- **Wear:** in the drawing, the coat's hem torn into a ragged notch, a sole flapping at the toe and the umbrella's broken spoke; scuffed is two pale scuffs on the coat, scorched two soot marks there, both tones of the coat.
