# The team shooter's engineer

- **Kind:** `engineer` · **Name:** Wrenchie
- **A stranger should say:** "the engineer with the hard hat, the goggles, the wrench and his little turret."
- **Silhouette:** as tall as the other grown men (the miner, the hunter, the merc), broad and square, a slab of a chest on long legs, and a small head for his body (about a quarter of his height with the hat, where the plumber's is near half). The head is a dome hard hat with a narrow rim sticking out all round it, front and back alike, never a cap's bill out front, over big round goggles. His kit is carried low and clear of the head: a little gun turret hanging by its handle beside his back knee, to the side of his legs (a round body, a long barrel pointing back, three thin legs splayed under it), from a big steel robot hand, and one huge open-jawed wrench held low in the other hand, slanting forward, its jaw out in front of his knees.
- **Traits:** a dome hard hat with a rim all round; big dark welding goggles down over his eyes, so no eye shows; a clean-shaven square jaw, jutting forward, no moustache; one robot hand, a steel gauntlet to the elbow; a work shirt; khaki trousers with a belt; one leather work glove; the little turret he builds, which no other kind carries; a huge wrench with one big open jaw. Not a lantern jaw as wide as the dome: on a small head it makes the head big again.
- **Palette:** costume (the player's): the shirt; the turret a dark tone of it (−24), far enough apart to stay two shapes on a light ground and in the mine. The hard hat is neutral steel `#c9c5bc`: in a light tone of the shirt it matched the costume the way the plumber's cap does. - **Gait:** trudges, heavy-stepped, the turret and the wrench swinging at his sides.
- **The nod:** the little turret, carried low (the read nobody else has, and what names him: the clean read took him for a plumber with a toolbox until it was there); the goggles down over his eyes (no eye shows, the original's face); the dome hard hat (the head shape and the trade, its rim all round where the plumber's cap has a bill); the robot hand; the huge wrench; the tall, square build with a small head (what keeps him from the plumber); the square jaw (the gruff Texan, at 20 px); the work shirt and khakis (the colour block).
