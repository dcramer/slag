# The limbless hero

- **Kind:** `limbless` · **Name:** Raymond
- **A stranger should say:** "the hero with no arms or legs."
- **Silhouette:** a small round body and a big-nosed head with a spiky hair tuft standing up, and nothing joining it to its white gloves and big shoes: each floats clear of the body with background all round it.
- **Traits:** no arms or legs; big white gloves; big shoes; a big nose; a spiky hair tuft he can spin; a hooded top with a round mark of ours on the chest (never the original's); a neckerchief.
- **Palette:** costume (the player's, `#7b3fa6` on the sheets): the top; the shoes a light tone of it (+24). Gloves white `#f2efe6`, the chest ring cream `#efe7cf`, skin `#f2c9a0` (the nose with it), hair `#8a5a34`, the neckerchief a linen `#e4ddd0`, eyes `#2a1f1a`.
- **Gait:** the shoes step on their own under him, the gloves swing ahead and behind; in the air the hair tuft spins.
- **The nod:** the gaps where the limbs should be (the read: nobody else has them); the floating gloves and shoes; the hair tuft standing up; the big nose.
- **Our twist:** a knock-off: the gloves are washing-up gloves with their cuffs rolled, bleached white, one shoe is a size bigger than the other with its toe curled up, and a safety pin holds the neckerchief.
- **Swaps:** a plaster across the cheek; a price tag on one glove; a sticker on the top.
- **Palette alts:** the hair in brown `#8a5a34`, straw `#c99a4a` or charcoal `#4a3c36`.
- **Idles and cheers:** standing; chin up and smug, the toe tapping. Both gloves flung up high in a grin; a thumbs up.
- **Gait range:** 0.1–0.7 px of extra bounce on each step, a lean of up to 0.04.
- **Wear:** scuffed is a faded patch on the top and a pale scuff on a shoe; scorched is soot low on the top and a mark on a shoe.
- **Rig:** drawn flat: a game-size design worn with rolled glove cuffs, a bigger curled shoe, the safety pin and a bent hair spike; eleven poses (`art/sprites/limbless/design.prompt.txt`). The gaps are the read, and the image model draws them about a world px wide, which the cast's pen closes at play size (in the first build the gloves and shoes read as attached). So every drawing goes through `art/sprites/limbless/spread.py`, which slides each floating piece straight out (the shoes down together, a glove sideways) until about 2 world px of background part it from the body; three poses that laid a glove over the head or body were redrawn from the spread design, which drew its gaps as wide. Gloves are `loose` (they wave about from pose to pose), so `body` sizes him by the rest; he stands on his shoes. The nose is the skin's role: its underside and the jaw's shade are one tan, and in a role of its own the jaw traced as a crescent of nose. The hair's shade has its own family, or it traced as a streak of the player's colour (the shoes' tone) through the tuft. The neck hole's ink under the face is folded away (`min.dark`), so nothing dark reads as a mouth. Two fresh readers: "Rayman (limbless hero), medium-high. Head, hands and feet float apart with no limbs between them, clearest in the run and jump" and "limbless hero (medium-high): hands, feet and head float as separate pieces with gaps between them, clearest in the jump"; neither paired him with anyone.
- **Concept prompt:** A cartoon hero with no arms and no legs: a small round body in a hooded top with a round badge, a big-nosed head with a spiky tuft of hair standing up, a neckerchief held with a safety pin, and big white rubber gloves and two big mismatched shoes floating clear of the body with space all round them.
