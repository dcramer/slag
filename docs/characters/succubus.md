# The succubus with bat wings on her head

A kind of the cast: [CHARACTERS.md](../CHARACTERS.md) has the rules every kind follows.

- **Kind:** `succubus` · **Name:** Moribund
- **A stranger should say:** "the succubus from the fighting game, the one with the bat wings on her head."
- **Silhouette:** slim and long-legged, standing tall with a hand on her hip: two bat wings sprouting from either side of her head and spread up and out like a crest, a second, bigger pair of bat wings at her back, long hair in a big wave down her back, a strapless bodysuit, thigh-high boots. Head wings first: they must stand clear of the hair with background between, or they're ears.
- **Traits:** the bat wings on her head; the bat wings at her back; the long big hair; the strapless bodysuit with a heart cut; the thigh-high boots; a flirty, cocky pose.
- **Palette:** the bodysuit in the player's colour, the head wings a light of it and the felt back wings a dark; the hair a dark neutral (charcoal, near black or a dark brown, its colourways), the skin, the black tights, the cream slippers and the hangers' wire neutral.
- **Color slot:** costume: the bodysuit; the head wings a light of it, the back wings a dark.
- **Gait:** a strutting walk on her toes; her jump is a wing flap; she floats a beat at the top.
- **The nod:** the head wings (the read); the back wings; the hair and the long legs.
- **Our twist:** she's a party-shop costume: the back wings are felt on bent wire coat hangers, one drooping, the head wings a hair clip set, and she wears fuzzy slippers instead of boots, one of them a bunny.
- **Swaps:** which wing droops; a slipper lost; a hanger hook poking up.
- **Rig:** drawn flat (2026-11-06, `art/sprites/succubus/design.prompt.txt`), three-quarter view, 27.6 px tall with the head wings, the air clip at 0.82, the wall at 0.9 and the knockout at 0.76 inside the shared box. Twelve images, 24 credits: two designs, two wear edits, eight pose sheets (four prompts, three poses to each but the wall and knockout two to one, each drawn twice and cut). The wear edit drooped the back wing off its hanger, set a hanger's hook over her back shoulder, tore the other wing and made the front slipper a bunny; the pose sheets made both slippers bunnies, which doesn't show at play size. Her drawn eyes are thinner than the ink, so the near eye is set by hand where each drawing has it (`eyes.at`), the face kit's `heavy` eye, half-lidded and cocky, the far one a sliver. The knockout drawing is tipped 4° (`art/sprites/succubus/prep.py`): level, its flat underside faced straight down, where the silhouette's ink is thickest, and reached under the floor. A plaster on the cheek is her swap. Design read, design-1 black at 28 px beside the cast's blind sheet, two fresh readers: "the bat-winged succubus (Morrigan from Darkstalkers), medium. Slim female figure, bat wings off the head or back, wings spread"; "a succubus, maybe Morrigan — medium — bat wings, horns, slim female figure". The blind sheet, two fresh readers on 38: "succubus (Morrigan, Darkstalkers), medium: bat wings, horns, slim female figure"; "succubus (Morrigan from Darkstalkers), medium: bat wings on the head and back, slim figure." Neither paired her with another kind; passed on the first sheet. The head wings carried it, wide and fanned on a tall slim figure with background between them and her hair; one reader in each round also said horns, so they stay fanned and ribbed.
- **Concept prompt:** A slim long-legged cartoon woman standing side on with a hand on her hip, two bat wings growing from the sides of her head spread up and out like a crest, a second larger pair of bat wings on her back made of felt on bent wire coat hangers with one drooping, long big wavy hair down her back, a strapless bodysuit with a heart-shaped cut, fuzzy slippers instead of boots, one a bunny slipper, cocky smile.
