# The green block creeper

A kind of the cast: [CHARACTERS.md](../CHARACTERS.md) has the rules every kind follows.

- **Kind:** `creeper` · **Name:** Creepah
- **A stranger should say:** "the green blocky creeper that sneaks up on you."
- **Silhouette:** a tall upright block for a body with a square block head the same width on top, four short square legs under it, two at the front and two at the back, and no arms at all; a frowning face of square holes.
- **Traits:** a square head and a tall square body, the same width; four stubby block legs; no arms; a face of four square holes, two eyes and a frown; a lit fuse.
- **Palette:** the body and head in the player's colour, its patches a light and a dark of it; the face holes a dark `#1e2a1e`, the fuse and candle neutral.
- **Color slot:** costume: the body.
- **Gait:** a shuffling sidle on four stiff legs, leaning in; it swells and shakes before a jump.
- **The nod:** the block body on four legs with no arms (the outline: the miner has arms and a wider head, the impostor a bean); the frowning square face.
- **Our twist:** it's a cardboard box painted in squares, with a birthday candle stuck on its head for a fuse, the flame lit.
- **Swaps:** a dented corner; a strip of tape over a split; the candle burnt down short.
- **Rig:** to draw flat (2026-11-05).
- **Concept prompt:** A cartoon creature made from a tall cardboard box painted in a patchwork of green squares, seen in three-quarter view, a square block head the same width as its tall block body, four short square block legs under it, no arms at all, a face of four black square holes, two eyes and a frowning mouth, a lit birthday candle stuck on top of its head for a fuse, a dented corner patched with tape.
