# The green block creeper

A kind of the cast: [CHARACTERS.md](../CHARACTERS.md) has the rules every kind follows.

- **Kind:** `creeper` · **Name:** Creepbox
- **A stranger should say:** "the green blocky creeper that sneaks up on you."
- **Silhouette:** a tall upright block for a body with a square block head the same width on top, four short square legs under it, two at the front and two at the back, and no arms at all; a frowning face of square holes cut right through him.
- **Traits:** a square head and a tall square body, the same width; four stubby block legs; no arms; a face of four square holes, two eyes and a frown; a lit fuse.
- **Palette:** the body and head in the player's colour, its painted squares a light (+22) and a dark (−20) of it; the packing tape `#c49a5a`, the torn corner's corrugated card `#9e7e49`, the candle's wax `#f0ebda` and flame `#f4b63a` neutral. The face holes are holes: the level shows through them at play size, dark on the dark themes and the lobby cards.
- **Color slot:** costume: the body.
- **Gait:** a shuffling sidle on four stiff legs, then a scurry tipped forward; it swells up fat and shakes on the cheer.
- **The nod:** the block body on four legs with no arms (the outline: the miner has arms and a wider head, the impostor a bean, the sneak is a box on two legs, wider than tall); the frowning square face (in black his outline alone is a post, so the face is cut through him and the silhouette carries it).
- **Our twist:** it's a cardboard box painted in squares, packing tape round a split in its middle, a corner torn open to the corrugated brown, a dent knocked into the top of its head, and a birthday candle burnt down to a stub on its head for a fuse, the flame lit, the wax dripping.
- **Swaps:** a plaster on the crown.
- **Palette alts:** the packing tape in brown `#c49a5a`, grey duct tape `#a4a8ac` or masking tape `#e4d6ae`.
- **Idles and cheers:** the idle from the design; leaning back, the head tipped up to look at the flame. Swelled up round to burst, shaking, the eyes shut; standing tall, then stretched up off his feet.
- **Gait range:** a bounce of 0.1–0.5 px on each step, a lean of up to 0.03.
- **Wear:** in the drawing, the tape over the split, the torn corner and the dented head; scuffed is a faded streak on the box, scorched a soot streak.
- **Rig:** drawn flat (`art/sprites/creeper/design.prompt.txt`), eleven poses three to a sheet, 27 images. The design came back as the original redrawn, so the twist is in the wear edit. Read as a black silhouette his outline is a post ("a thin tall slab with a small antenna/spike on top", twice), so the face's four holes are cut through the frame (`art/sprites/creeper/holes.py`, `map.holes`) and drawn a fifth bigger than the model drew them, which were specks at play size; the kit's `cutout` eye draws nothing over a hole, acts in the brows and shuts it with the box's colour. Blind read 1 (tall candle): "Minecraft Creeper; medium. Tall, narrow, blocky upright body with face holes"; "Minecraft Creeper. Low-medium. Tall narrow block with face holes, no arms", the second adding "21, 23 and 25 read as one blocky family" (the sneak and the miner). Read 2, at 24 px: "Black Mage: low. Tall narrow robe with a pointed top and visible eye holes"; "unsure. Low guess the green block creeper: tall narrow upright column with a small point on top". The candle's spike read as an antenna, a fuse and a hat, so it burnt down to a stub (an edit of each sheet). Read 3: "Minecraft Creeper (medium): tall narrow block with a dark face, no arms"; "Minecraft creeper, medium. Tall narrow block body with face holes and short stubby legs". Neither paired him with another kind.
- **Concept prompt:** A cartoon creature made from a tall cardboard box painted in a patchwork of green squares, seen in three-quarter view, a square block head the same width as its tall block body, four short square block legs under it, no arms at all, a face of four black square holes, two eyes and a frowning mouth, a lit birthday candle burnt down to a stub on top of its head for a fuse, a dented corner, packing tape round a split.
