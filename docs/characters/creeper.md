# The green block creeper

A kind of the cast: [CHARACTERS.md](../CHARACTERS.md) has the rules every kind follows.

- **Kind:** `creeper` · **Name:** Creepbox
- **A stranger should say:** "the green blocky creeper that sneaks up on you."
- **Silhouette:** a tall upright box for a body with a big cube head the same width on top, every corner sharp, four short square feet under it, and no arms at all; seen nearly from the front, a big black frowning face square to us.
- **Traits:** a square head and a tall square body, the same width, sharp-cornered; four stubby block feet; no arms; a big black face, two square eyes over a long down-turned mouth; a skin of pixel tiles; a lit fuse.
- **Palette:** the body and head in the player's colour, its pixel tiles a light (+20) and a dark (−20) of it; the face solid ink; the torn corner's corrugated card `#9e7e49`, the candle's wax `#f0ebda` and flame `#f4b63a` neutral.
- **Color slot:** costume: the body.
- **Gait:** a shuffling sidle on four stiff feet, then a scurry tipped forward.
- **The nod:** the block body on four feet with no arms, square at every corner (the outline: the miner has arms and a wider head, the impostor a bean, the sneak is a box on two legs, wider than tall; round, he reads as a cylinder); the big black frowning face, solid ink and square to us, so it reads in any player's colour; the pixel tiles, in tones of his colour (the homage's skin by its shape, not its green).
- **Our twist:** it's a cardboard box painted in pixel squares, a corner torn open to the corrugated brown, the top corner of its head crumpled in, and a birthday candle stuck in crooked on its head for a fuse, burnt to a stub, the flame lit. No tape: a band round his middle read as a stripe.
- **Swaps:** a plaster on the crown.
- **Palette alts:** the candle's wax in white `#f0ebda`, beeswax `#e6d0a0` or grey `#c8cacb`.
- **Idles and cheers:** the idle from the design; leaning back, the head tipped up to look at the flame. The candle flaring up tall; standing up tall and proud. Never swelled round: round, he reads as a cylinder.
- **Gait range:** a bounce of 0.1–0.5 px on each step, a lean of up to 0.03.
- **Wear:** in the drawing, the torn corner, the crumpled head and the crooked candle; scuffed is a faded streak on the box, scorched a soot streak.
- **Rig:** drawn flat, redrawn 2026-11-06 for the clean colour read (`art/sprites/creeper/design.prompt.txt`), eleven poses three to a sheet, 17 images. The first drawing (three-quarter on, the face four holes cut through him, packing tape round his middle; 27 images, its passes from readers who could see the commit log) read in the clean colour audit as "Unsure. Magenta striped cylinder", "pink cylinder with a wick on top" and "Pink cylinder-shaped body with stripes": the holes showed the level, a light patch; the tape was a stripe, the shaded side a cylinder's. Mock, the old drawings with the face inked solid and the tape painted out: "Creeper (Minecraft, blue) — medium — tall blocky body with pixel-pattern skin and short legs"; "Creeper (Minecraft) — low — tall blocky body on four stubby feet", pairing him with the sneak's green box. So a new design, face on, the face big and solid ink, pixel tiles all over and nothing across the box. Its design read (the design as every pose): "Creeper (Minecraft), medium, tall block body with the frowning face, recoloured blue"; "Creeper (Minecraft, blue) — medium — tall block body, pixel frowning face, tiny feet"; the wax drips over the head's edges traced as horns, so an edit took them off. Blind read, in colour: "Creeper, Minecraft, recoloured (high): tall blocky body, pixel face and four stubby feet"; "Creeper (blue recolour) – medium – tall block body, pixel face, four short legs". Neither paired him with another kind; both left the sneak's box "unsure".
- **Concept prompt:** A cartoon creature made from a tall cardboard box painted all over in a grid of big square pixel tiles in three greens, seen almost straight from the front, a big cube head the same width as its tall box body, every corner sharp, four short square block feet under it, no arms at all, a big solid black frowning face, two square eyes over a long down-turned mouth, a lit birthday candle burnt down to a stub stuck in crooked on top of its head for a fuse, the head's top corner crumpled in, a bottom corner torn open to the corrugated brown.
