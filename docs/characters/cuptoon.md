# The cup-headed toon

- **Kind:** `cuptoon` · **Name:** Mughead
- **A stranger should say:** "the cartoon guy with a cup for a head."
- **Silhouette:** a cup for a head, wider at the top than at its foot and wider than the body under it, a lid's lip proud of its rim, a loop handle out the back and a tall straw kinked at the top standing out of it; a tiny rubber-hose body under it.
- **Traits:** a cup head with a loop handle; a striped straw; big black pie-cut eyes; white gloves; high-waisted shorts; big round shoes; black hose limbs that bend in curves; drawn in a 1930s cartoon spirit.
- **Palette:** costume (the player's): the shorts; the straw's stripes drawn in it, the cardboard sleeve a light of it (+14). Paper cup, lid's lip, handle and gloves `#f4f1ea`, plastic lid `#5c5a62`, tape `#a9a8a4`, coffee `#5c3a26`, hose limbs and neck `#1c1a24`, shoes `#6b4a35`.
- **Gait:** a bouncy rubber-hose strut with arms swinging in loops; a jig for a second cheer.
- **The nod:** the cup head with its handle (the one head nobody else has; without the handle it read as a box); the straw (it breaks the top of the outline); big pie-cut eyes, black hose limbs and white gloves (the 1930s cartoon read, which is half the joke); the high-waisted shorts (the toon costume, and the colour block).
- **Our twist:** no china: a takeaway coffee cup. A paper cup under a grey plastic lid knocked crooked, coffee showing under it, a cardboard sleeve in the player's colour round its foot, a crumpled dent in its back, and a mug's loop handle taped onto the paper with two strips of tape, because the knock-off couldn't afford a real one.
- **Swaps:** a sticking plaster on one cheek.
- **Palette alts:** the shoes in brown leather `#6b4a35`, tan `#9a7448` or grey `#6e6a66`.
- **Idles and cheers:** the idle from the model; gloves on his hips, rocking on his heels. Both gloves thrown up; a jig with one leg kicked high.
- **Gait range:** a bounce of 0.2–0.85 px on each step, a lean of up to 0.03.
- **Wear:** scuffed is a faded streak on the shorts, scorched a soot streak there; the cup, the face, stays clean.
- **Rig:** drawn flat, worn with the crooked lid, the dented cup, the taped handle and the flapping sole. Eleven poses.
