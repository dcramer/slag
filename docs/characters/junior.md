# Junior, the one-eyed robot in the backwards cap

A kind of the cast: [CHARACTERS.md](../CHARACTERS.md) has the rules every kind follows. Junior is the one kind that is no homage: it is Sentry's own robot, which the owner's team made, seated as an easter egg (Decisions, 2026-11-06). It keeps its own name and look; there is no parody twist to add.

- **Kind:** `junior` · **Name:** Junior · **Egg:** never rolled like the rest; about one look in a hundred is Junior (`EGGS`, `EGG_CHANCE` in `src/gen/character.ts`), and a lobby never forces anyone onto it.
- **Reference:** `art/sprites/junior/reference.png` (https://junior.sentry.dev/junior-character.png). Draw from it: the design is set, so the first Tripo image is an edit of the reference, not a new design.
- **A stranger should say:** "the little yellow robot with one big eye and a backwards cap." Someone who knows Sentry should say "Junior".
- **Silhouette:** a square yellow box head as wide as the shoulders, a purple cap on it backwards with its bill out behind, two zigzag antennae with ball tips springing up and out, round headphone ears; a pink hoodie; one long bendy arm ending in a big three-finger claw, the other holding up a phone; thin purple legs with yellow ankle bands, white socks and chunky purple roller-skate shoes.
- **Traits:** the one huge round eye; the big toothy grin; the backwards cap; the zigzag antennae; the claw hand; the phone playing music; the roller skates.
- **Palette:** the hoodie in the player's colour (pink by default); the cap and the skates a dark of it; the antenna and the wheels a light steel, apart from both the cap and the headphones; the head, arms and ankle bands Junior's own yellow, its skin (owner, 2026-11-06: skin is a neutral, so it stays); the legs, the headphones' rims, the phone and the claw's joints neutral (dark purple-grey as a neutral); white socks, eye white and teeth neutral.
- **Color slot:** costume: the hoodie; the cap and shoes a dark of it.
- **Swaps:** a strip of grey tape across the cheek, or none.
- **Wear:** the hoodie's hem torn, grey tape round the claw arm's elbow (the design); a scuff or a scorch on the hoodie (the roll).
- **Palette alts:** the legs, joints and headphones in a purple-grey, a steel or a warm grey.
- **Idles and cheers:** standing with the phone up; looking at it, tapping the screen. The claw up snapping and the phone up filming; a wink and a grin, filming it.
- **Gait range:** a bounce of 0–0.5 px on each step, a lean of up to 0.03.
- **Gait:** rolls on the skates, a glide between pushes; jumps with the antennae springing; the claw hand clacks on a cheer and the phone is held up to film it.
- **The nod:** it's the original, so all of it: the box head with the one eye, the backwards cap, the antennae, the claw, the phone.
- **Rig:** drawn flat (2026-11-06) in two rounds (art/sprites/junior/design.prompt.txt). Round 1 drew the reference's own sizes: in black it paired with nobody once the jump stopped springing the antennae up as rabbit ears ("small, spindly figure with things sticking out of its head; unsure"), but in colour beside the reference the antennae read as "bone-shaped blobs", the claw as "round yellow mitts" and the phone not at all. Round 2 draws the props bigger than the reference's: a pincer as big as the head, a phone as tall as it, steel wheels, a longer bill, and steel zigzag antennae. With all three antennae, colour readers saw "antlers, bones or twigs" and black readers "a small kid with spiky hair" (paired with the mercenary) and "a small thin imp… with pointed ears or horns" (paired with the succubus); so only the one rising from the cap is kept (prune.py). What ships, in colour: the eye, the claw ("reads well as a big yellow pincer") and the skates ("dark skates with visible wheels") yes; the phone yes or partly; the grin, the cap's backwards bill and the zigzag ("one grey stalk above the cap. The zigzag shape is gone") partly. In black: "small thin figure with a pointed cap", unpaired, and "small, skinny figure with a pointed cap, like an elf", paired with the elf. Not passed; the owner's call.
- **Concept prompt:** the reference image itself, redrawn in the cast's flat style, side on in three-quarter, every trait above kept.
