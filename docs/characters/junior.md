# Junior, the one-eyed robot in the backwards cap

A kind of the cast: [CHARACTERS.md](../CHARACTERS.md) has the rules every kind follows. Junior is the one kind that is no homage: it is Sentry's own robot, which the owner's team made, seated as an easter egg (Decisions, 2026-11-06). It keeps its own name and look; there is no parody twist to add.

- **Kind:** `junior` · **Name:** Junior · **Egg:** never rolled like the rest; about one look in a hundred is Junior (`EGGS`, `EGG_CHANCE` in `src/gen/character.ts`), and a lobby never forces anyone onto it.
- **Reference:** `art/sprites/junior/reference.png` (https://junior.sentry.dev/junior-character.png). Draw from it: the design is set, so the first Tripo image is an edit of the reference, not a new design.
- **A stranger should say:** "the little yellow robot with one big eye and a backwards cap." Someone who knows Sentry should say "Junior".
- **Silhouette:** a square yellow box head as wide as the shoulders, a purple cap on it backwards with its bill out behind, two zigzag antennae with ball tips springing up and out, round headphone ears; a pink hoodie; one long bendy arm ending in a big three-finger claw, the other holding up a phone; thin purple legs with yellow ankle bands, white socks and chunky purple roller-skate shoes.
- **Traits:** the one huge round eye; the big toothy grin; the backwards cap; the zigzag antennae; the claw hand; the phone playing music; the roller skates.
- **Palette:** the hoodie in the player's colour (pink by default); the cap and the shoes a dark of it; the head, arms and ankle bands Junior's own yellow (a fixed tone the read needs); the legs, the headphones' rims, the phone and the claw's joints neutral (dark purple-grey as a neutral); white socks, eye white and teeth neutral.
- **Color slot:** costume: the hoodie; the cap and shoes a dark of it.
- **Gait:** rolls on the skates, a glide between pushes; jumps with the antennae springing; the claw hand clacks on a cheer and the phone is held up to film it.
- **The nod:** it's the original, so all of it: the box head with the one eye, the backwards cap, the antennae, the claw, the phone.
- **Rig:** to draw flat (2026-11-06) from the reference. The blind read doesn't apply (no stranger knows it); the check is the owner's eye and the cast's pairs: the robot boy, the creeper and the miner's square heads, the mech.
- **Concept prompt:** the reference image itself, redrawn in the cast's flat style, side on in three-quarter, every trait above kept.
