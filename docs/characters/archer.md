# The red-braided girl with the bow who hunts robot beasts

A kind of the cast: [CHARACTERS.md](../CHARACTERS.md) has the rules every kind follows.

- **Kind:** `archer` · **Name:** Aloof
- **A stranger should say:** "the girl with the red braids and the bow who hunts robot dinosaurs."
- **Silhouette:** side on, an athletic young woman in a low hunter's crouch, a bow drawn and held out level ahead with background all round it, thick braided hair bushing out behind her head, a quiver of arrows over her back, tribal leathers with a fur shoulder wrap, wrapped legs and boots. The drawn bow ahead is the read: nobody else in the cast holds one.
- **Traits:** the drawn bow held out level; the thick red braids; the fur shoulder wrap; the quiver; the leather and rope outfit; the glowing focus at her temple.
- **Palette:** the leathers and wraps in the player's colour, the fur a light of it; the hair red-brown (a neutral the read needs, like the slayer's white), the skin, the bow's wood and the quiver neutral.
- **Color slot:** costume: the leathers; the fur a light of it.
- **Gait:** a light, low hunter's stalk; her jump is a forward roll; the bow stays in hand.
- **The nod:** the bow drawn ahead (the read); the braids bushing out behind; the fur wrap.
- **Our twist:** her quarry is household robots: the bow is a bent curtain rod strung with a bungee cord, the arrows are suction-cup darts, and the focus at her temple is a bottle cap with a sticker in it.
- **Swaps:** a dart stuck to her own boot; a braid frayed loose; a fur tuft missing.
- **Rig:** to draw flat (2026-11-06). Pairs to watch: the raider (a slim woman with a braid: the bow and the bushy braids must carry her apart), the elf and the hunter (both hold something ahead: hers is a thin curve with a string, never a block), the fighter's stance.
- **Concept prompt:** A side-on cartoon young woman in a low hunter's crouch, drawing a bow held out level ahead, the bow a bent curtain rod strung with a bungee cord, a suction-cup dart on the string, thick bushy red braided hair puffing out behind her head, a fur wrap on one shoulder, tribal leather and rope outfit, a quiver of suction-cup darts on her back, wrapped legs and boots, a bottle cap at her temple, focused and calm.
