# The pink puffball

- **Kind:** `puffball` · **Name:** Squirby
- **A stranger should say:** "the little pink puffball", and then "a cheap knock-off of it."
- **Silhouette:** a near-perfect ball on two big oval feet, with stubby round arms.
- **Traits:** blush marks; a tiny mouth; big feet. The eyes are ours: two mismatched googly craft eyes, not the original's tall ovals.
- **Palette:** costume (the player's, pink by default): the ball and its arms, because the original comes in every colour; the feet a dark of it (tone −18), the toe scuffed through a step lighter (−8). The googly eyes a white `#fbf8f2` round ink pupils `#2a1f1a` (the face kit's `googly`); blush `#ec7a94`; plaster `#e9d3b0`.
- **Gait:** toddles on its big feet; puffs up round and floats in the air.
- **The nod:** the ball (the whole body, its outline the original's: anything stuck on it moves the read); the big feet, darker than the ball (they make it a creature, and part it from the dot-eater); a sweet face, the blush and a small smile.
- **Our twist:** a cheap knock-off with stuck-on googly craft eyes: two white discs, mismatched (the one toward the front much the bigger), round loose pupils that sit askew, one sunk to the bottom and one up in its corner. They never shut: the pupils fly back into the run, drop on the way up, float to the top on a fall and roll up on a cheer, and a knockout scratches a cross on each disc.
- **Swaps:** a price sticker on its side; a scuff of shelf dust; a bite mark from a dog.
- **Palette alts:** the plaster in fabric tan `#e9d3b0`, gauze `#f1ede4` or grey tape `#b9bcbf`.
- **Idles and cheers:** the idle's sway of the arms; looking up, arms behind it, tapping one toe. Both arms up, a big open smile; puffed up round, waving one arm.
- **Gait range:** a toddle's bounce of 0.1–0.8 px on each step, a lean of up to 0.03.
- **Wear:** drawn in: a bite out of the back arm and the front toe scuffed pale. Scuffed is a pale streak on the ball; scorched is a soot streak on its side.
- **Rig:** drawn flat. The first twist was a cheap vinyl squeeze toy (a moulding seam, a squeaker valve, a mould number): the seam and a tape cross weren't drawn (at play size one read as a mouth, the other as a third eye), the valve folded into the ball, and what was left was the original's own shape and face, the original redrawn (owner, 2026-11-06). First flat read: both readers at medium. With a plaster cross on the crown: "unsure" and low, "the small bumps on top make it less clear"; with the cross gone (the plaster now on the cheek), both name it again, at medium and low. The second twist (2026-11-06) was drawn as a claw-machine keychain with googly eyes, a steel keyring on its crown; read black at play size before any pose, the ring on a stalk was "Chao (Sonic Adventure), low", and set straight on the crown, "a walking bomb with a fuse, low-medium": the ring was cut, and the eyes made mismatched. In colour, that design read "a Kirby parody … mismatched googly eyes" (high). The drawings' pupils sit at two heights, which the eye finder never pairs, so each is painted out and a dot set in its disc's middle (`art/sprites/puffball/eyes.py`; the unpainted drawings in `drawn/`); the knocked-out drawing's shut discs are painted white with an ink cross. The toe scuff (−8) is one pale with the eyes' whites in the drawing: the whites go as specks under the kit's eyes. The plaster sits low on the side behind the near eye: beside the googly eye it was a brow. The biggest drawn disc pokes past the ball's outline, so its white goes as a speck up to 30 world px² and the kit's far eye is drawn 2.3 times the near one's (with the kit's 0.85), over all of it: smaller, a ring of the disc showed round it like a monocle. The blush is one solid pink (see-through, it went grey on a green or teal ball). The blind read of the shipped sheet: "Kirby or a Boo-like ghost, low. Wide round blob; the third pose has a tooth or ghost shape" and "Kirby, medium. Round body, big feet", each pairing it only with the four undrawn kinds' round stand-ins. In colour (the variety sheet and the 3× scene): "a lumpy, scruffy, off-brand Kirby … mismatched googly eyes, one small and one huge, bulging off the side of its head … a dopey, battered and slightly melted cousin of Kirby, not the cute, pristine original" and "a lumpy, slightly melted Kirby that someone squished out of clay … one big googly bulging eye … a dazed, derpy look instead of Kirby's two tall, earnest ovals".
- **Concept prompt:** A near-perfect round ball creature on two big oval red feet, with stubby round arms, blush marks and a tiny smile. It is a cheap knock-off: its eyes are two stuck-on googly craft eyes, white plastic discs of two sizes with loose black pupils rolled two different ways.
