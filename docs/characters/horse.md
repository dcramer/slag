# The barnyard party game's horse

A kind of the cast: [CHARACTERS.md](../CHARACTERS.md) has the rules every kind follows.

- **Kind:** `horse` · **Name:** Hoarse
- **A stranger should say:** "the horse from the chicken-horse game."
- **Silhouette:** on all four legs, seen from the side: a long barrel body on four long legs with its back level, a long neck slanting forward from the chest with the mane standing up along its top as a crest, a long blunt head out in front pointing down, and a long tail of straight hair hanging from the rump to the heels.
- **Traits:** a long boxy muzzle, as deep at the nose as at the eyes, with a pale nose, a big nostril and buck teeth; the mane as a crest along the neck; a long straight tail, never a brush; flat-bottomed hooves wider than the legs; a goofy buck-toothed grin.
- **Palette:** the costume in the player's colour (drawn `#c8743a`), the far legs a dark of it (tone −14) and the scarf a darker one (−28); the rest neutral: nose and muzzle `#e6cfae`; mane, tail, hooves and nostril `#4a3428`; zip and knee patch `#9a9c98`; an open mouth's tongue `#9a3d36`.
- **Color slot:** costume: the coat, head and all; the far legs and the scarf in darks of it.
- **Gait:** a high-stepping trot on the walk, a gallop stretched out and gathered on the run, a show jumper's leap and a buck in the air.
- **The nod:** the four legs under a long body, side on (only a horse's outline: upright on two legs, ten blind readers took it for a fox or the bandicoot); the long neck with its crest of mane and the long blunt head pointing down (a horse, not a dog: a dog has no neck and a pointed snout); the long straight tail hanging to the heels and the hooves (a horse, not a fox's brush); the goofy grin (its personality, at card scale).
- **Our twist:** it's a cheap costume. A zip runs down its chest from under the scarf, stuck open at the bottom, a grey patch is sewn on one front knee, and it's lost its voice: a knitted scarf wound round its neck.
- **Swaps:** a sticking plaster on the jowl. The lozenge tin, the hand-sewn patch and the party horn are dropped: the tin is a speck at play scale, a horn in its mouth would stick out of the silhouette (swaps never change it), and a patch is already sewn on its knee in every drawing, where a second one rolled on would only read as more wear.
- **Palette alts:** the mane, tail and hooves together in dark bay `#4a3428`, black `#302c2c` or chestnut `#74492f`.
- **Idles and cheers:** the idle from the design; pawing the ground with one front hoof, smug. Rearing up on its hind legs, front hooves pawing the air; a buck, both hind legs kicked high behind.
- **Gait range:** a bounce of 0.2–0.9 px on each step, a lean of up to 0.03.
- **Wear:** in the drawing, the zip stuck open and a stitched grey patch on one front knee; scuffed is a faded patch on the body, scorched a soot mark there.
- **Rig:** first drawn flat upright on two legs (2026-11-04): a game-size design with the scarf, zip and patch added in a wear edit (over the prompt's length the image model dropped the scarf) and the head tilted down and the tail narrowed in a second; eleven poses drawn three to a sheet (`art/sprites/horse/design.prompt.txt`). Each drawing's near pupil is repainted solid: drawn, a pupil was a ring of ink round its glint, taken for the line, and the far one sits on the outline. Blind reads (2026-11-04, ten fresh readers over five drawings): never named; read as Fox McCloud or a plain fox ("pointed snout and ears, bushy tail, stands upright on two legs", low) and as the bandicoot ("spiky tuft on the head, lanky", low to medium), as a level head, a shaggy mane, tall ears or a fanned tail made it a canine. In colour it reads as a horse at once. Redrawn on four legs the same day: a new game-size design side on, with the mane a stiff crest along a long neck and a blunt boxy muzzle, the long tail added in an edit (the design drew none) and the scarf, zip and knee patch in the wear edit; eleven poses drawn three to a 16:9 sheet, cut apart and scaled to the design's ink (`art/sprites/horse/cut.py`), every open eye's near pupil inked solid (`pupils.py`). The walk is a trot, the run a gallop stretched out and gathered (drawn at 0.86 of the body size, or its nose and tail leave the box), the air a leap and a buck (0.8: the buck's tail stands up past the box's top), the wall slide rearing against the wall with its front hooves braced on it, the cheers a rear and a buck, the knockout on its side with all four legs stiff in the air. Two fresh readers then named it at once: "Plain horse (maybe Epona), medium-high. Gallops on four legs with a flowing tail and long neck. Stands rearing up in row 1, leaps in row 3"; "Plain horse, medium. Horse head and neck, gallops on four legs in the run frame and rears in the jump. The standing pose looks oddly upright, almost sitting or centaur-like." Neither paired it with another kind when asked whether any two figures could be the same character. The standing pose was then redrawn (2026-11-04), since its neck stood nearly upright and readers saw it "almost sitting or centaur-like" and "rearing": with the design as the reference the image model kept the upright neck twice, so the idle and the pawing idle were drawn from the gallop's drawing (its neck already stretched forward) as a sheet of two, standing square with the back level and the neck at a natural slant (`art/sprites/horse/sheets/sheetE-2.png`). The new idle is `design.png`; both are centred on their box, as the nose now reaches past it over the feet. The hand-drawn fallback is a horse on four legs too.
- **Concept prompt:** A goofy cartoon horse standing on all four legs, seen from its side, with a long neck rising steeply from its chest, a short stiff dark mane standing up along the neck in one neat crest, a long blunt boxy head pointing forward and down with a pale cream nose, a big nostril and big flat buck teeth in a grin, a long straight dark tail hanging to its heels and flat dark hooves wider than its legs; it is clearly a cheap costume, with a silver zip down its chest stuck open at the bottom, a grey patch sewn on one knee and a red knitted scarf wound round its neck.
