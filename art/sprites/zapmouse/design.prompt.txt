How design.png and the pose drawings were made (sixth round, 2026-11-06). The clean colour audit read the shipped mouse in purple as "Unsure. Purple cat-like body with pointed ears", "purple horned creature" and "Purple cat-like body with a grey tail": its shorter ear was bent down and taped, so one ear stood alone (a horn), and its crumpled tin tail was a grey blob. This round stands both ears up in a V with big black tips, makes the tail a clean zigzag of flat tin, and the cheeks big. Every image was 2 credits (no free images left this month); the candidates and sheets are in r6/.
