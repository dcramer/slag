How design.png and the pose drawings were made (2026-11-05). Every image was 2 credits (no free images left this month): 12 images, 24 credits. Drafts are in drafts/, the pose sheets in sheets/, the figures cut from them as drawn in drawn/, and those through holes.py in flat/.

1. Design (`node tools/art3d/style.ts design marcher`, --count 2; drafts/design-1, design-2). Both came back as a flat pixel crab, front on, as asked. design-1 kept: the more stepped outline, the claws a raised U each.
   Design read, both drafts as black silhouettes 22 px tall with their eye holes cut, beside the impostor, jelly, creeper, hopper, sneak and zapmouse designs, two fresh readers: "2: Space Invaders crab invader, high confidence. Wide pixel body, two antennae, raised pincer arms, two eye holes and short splayed legs"; "2: crab invader from Space Invaders, high. Antennae on top, raised claw arms, eye holes, segmented legs." Both paired only the two drafts.

2. The twist and the wear, as one edit (`node tools/art3d/style.ts worn marcher`, --ref drafts/design-1.png, 2 drawn; drafts/worn-1 kept: the taped tile stands crooked between the antennae, the near antenna's tip hangs off; worn-2 drew two tiles floating loose):
The same character exactly as in the reference image: the same pose, design, proportions, colours, line and flat white background. Change only this, and make each change big and obvious: one square pixel tile on the top edge of its body, beside an antenna, has fallen out, leaving a square notch, and is stuck back on crooked, tilted at an angle and sticking up out of the outline, a strip of grey tape (#a4a8ac) across it; the near antenna is bent over sideways at its tip, its top tile hanging off at an angle. The two square eye holes, the raised claws and the legs stay exactly as they are. Nothing else changes, no dirt, no texture.

3. Poses, three to a 16:9 image with drafts/worn-1.png (the black-holed eyes) as the reference, 2 drawn each. Every sheet's prompt opens: "The same character exactly as in the reference image: the same design, proportions, colours, wear and tear, line and flat white background, in the same flat front view, upright, never tilted. Draw it three times side by side in one row: three separate figures of the same size, with wide white space between them, none touching. Every figure keeps its outline stepped square by square in big pixel tiles, both antennae (the left one bent over at the tip), the taped tile on top, the two square eye holes, and its two claws clear of the body." and ends: "Nothing else changes: no ground, no shadow, no motion lines, no text, no other figures."

4. Every figure was cut with `build.ts cut marcher SHEET NAME… --design` into flat/ (kept as drawn in drawn/), then through holes.py (`python3 -I art/sprites/marcher/holes.py IN OUT …`): the two black eye squares and their ink rim painted white, so `holes` cuts them through the frame; it prints the left eye's place for `eyes.at`. design.png is holes.py on drafts/worn-1.png.
