How design.png and the pose drawings were made (twist redraw, 2026-11-06: a takeaway coffee cup for a head, replacing the 2026-11-04 camping mug, whose chipped rim, tea-bag tag and flapping sole all shrank to specks at play size). Paid Studio images, 2 credits each: 20 images, 40 credits.

1. Design, as an edit of the 2026-11-04 camping-mug design (2 drawn, the first picked: the sleeve under the eyes, not across the smile):
The same character as in the reference image: the same small 1930s rubber-hose cartoon body and pose, the same high-waisted red shorts, big white gloves, big round brown shoes, black hose arms and legs, the same thin ink line, flat colours and flat white background. Change only his head, and make the change big: his whole head is now a big white paper takeaway coffee cup instead of a mug, with no handle at all. The cup is tall and tapers, clearly wider at the top than at the bottom, and wider than the body under it. On top sits a white plastic lid with a thick raised lip that sticks out past the cup's edge all round, and a low flat dome. A tall red-and-white striped straw pokes up out of a hole in the lid and bends over near its top. Round the lower half of the cup, a wide plain pale salmon cardboard sleeve (#e88a6e), a broad band that stands a little proud of the cup's sides. On the white front of the cup above the sleeve, the same two big black pie-cut cartoon eyes and a wide smile. No handle, no tea, no tag, no logo, no letters, no text anywhere. Nothing else changes, no dirt, no texture.

2. Wear (2 drawn, the first picked; it ignored "shorter and squatter"):
The same character exactly as in the reference image: the same pose, design, colours, line and flat white background. Change only this, and make each change big and obvious: the paper cup head is shorter and squatter, about as tall as it is wide at the top, still tapering and still wider than the body, so the red shorts, the gloves and the black hose legs under it are bigger; the white plastic lid has been knocked crooked and has popped up off the cup at the back, tilted, with a dark gap under its back edge; a big crumpled dent is pushed into the back edge of the paper cup, a notch in its outline; the striped straw is taller, chewed flat at the top and bent over sharply; the pale salmon sleeve has slipped down crooked to the bottom of the cup, below the eyes and the smile; the sole of one shoe has come loose and flaps open at the toe. The eyes, the smile and the front of the cup stay clean. No handle, no logo, no letters. Nothing else changes, no dirt, no texture.

3. By hand: the black gap under the popped lid painted coffee brown (a dark band over the eyes), and the head squashed to 0.74 of its height above the sleeve's foot. Its black silhouette at blind-sheet size read as "a boxy body with a curly antenna loop on top", then "a toaster-style robot": with no handle the cup is a box.

4. A steeper taper and arms held out (2 drawn, not picked: the lid's wide brim read as a sombrero):
... the cup tapers much more steeply ... the lid's lip sticks out well past the cup all round, a wide flat brim; the striped straw is twice as thick ... both arms are held out away from the body ...

5. A mug's handle taped onto the paper cup (2 drawn from step 3, the first picked):
The same character exactly as in the reference image: the same takeaway paper coffee cup head with its crooked popped-up white lid, coffee showing under it, the crumpled dent, the salmon cardboard sleeve, the same face, red shorts, white gloves, brown shoes with one flapping sole, the same line, flat colours and flat white background. Change only this, and make each change big and obvious: a big round loop handle, like a teacup's, has been stuck onto the back of the paper cup with two wide strips of grey tape across it; the handle sticks well out from the back of the cup with a big round hole in it; the striped straw is twice as thick, sticks straight up tall out of the lid and bends once near its top toward the front; a short black neck shows between the cup and the shorts; both arms are held a little away from the body in round hose curves, the big white gloves clear of the cup and the shorts. No logo, no letters. Nothing else changes, no dirt, no texture.
