How design.png and the pose drawings were made (2026-11-04). Images on paid Studio credits, 2 a picture.

1. Design (the `design` brief in tools/art3d/style.ts, before its last trim: the head pushed long, small neat ears, the mane flat; 4 drawn, the first picked): the cleanest horse silhouette, long head, flat mane, a tail hanging, dark hooves. The prompt ran past the image model's ~1,800 characters, and none drew the scarf.

2. Wear, with the twist added (2 drawn, the first picked: the clearer zip):
The same character exactly as in the reference image: the same pose, design, proportions, colours, line and flat white background. Change only this, and make each change big and obvious: a thick knitted red scarf (#c9473f) is wound round its throat under the head, one end hanging down its chest; a big silver zip runs straight up the middle of its belly from between its legs to the scarf, stuck open at the bottom, its two sides parted in a V over the lower belly; a big square patch of grey cloth is sewn on one knee with big stitches; the end of the tail is frayed into ragged strands. Nothing else changes, no dirt, no texture.

3. The read (2 drawn, the first picked): a level head over a fanned tail read as a fox standing up in the blind reads, so the head tilts down like a horse's and the tail narrows:
The same character exactly as in the reference image: the same pose, design, proportions, colours, line and flat white background. Change only this, and make each change big and obvious: its neck is longer, rising high out of the scarf, and the long head is tilted down so the muzzle points forward and down at a slant, like a real horse's; the tail is a smooth, narrow sweep of hair hanging straight down behind its legs, not frayed and not bushy. Nothing else changes, no dirt, no texture.

4. Poses as sheets, three to a 16:9 image (two for wall and death) with the design as the reference, each sheet drawn twice and the better kept, cut apart and scaled by one factor (the design's height over a standing figure's, 1.44) so every figure keeps its drawn scale: A (walk-1, walk-2, idle2), B (run-1, run-2, air-1), C (air-2, cheer, cheer2, with "one mouth only, the grin at the front end of the long muzzle": without it the cheers grinned twice, once on the cheek), D (wall, death). The prompt is each pose's line in tools/art3d/flat/kinds/horse.ts → poses, joined as "Draw it three times side by side in one row: three separate figures of the same size, with wide white space between them, none touching or overlapping, each in its own pose. Left: … Middle: … Right: …". Motion ticks and dust puffs are whitened out.

5. Pupils: each drawing's near pupil is repainted as a solid ink disc 22 px across. Drawn, a pupil is a ring of ink round a white glint, which the role map takes for the line, so the face kit found no eye, or took the far one, which sits hard against the outline.
