How design.png and the pose drawings were made (second round, on four legs, 2026-11-04). Upright on two legs, ten fresh blind readers over five drawings never named it (a fox, Fox McCloud, the bandicoot), so this round draws it side on, on four legs, with what only a horse has in outline: a long neck with the mane as a crest, a long blunt head, a long straight tail, hooves. Every image was 2 credits (no free images left); 14 images, 28 credits. The picked sheets are in sheets/; the first round's prompts are in git history.

1. Design (`node tools/art3d/style.ts design horse`, the second-round brief; 2 drawn, the second kept: a clean side-on horse with a stiff crest of mane and a blunt muzzle; the first came back blue with grey legs and a rope-loop tail). It drew no tail.

2. The tail (2 drawn from design-2, the second kept: the tail stands clear of the back leg, where the first's merged into one block with it):
The same character exactly as in the reference image: the same pose, design, proportions, colours, line, side view facing right and flat white background. Change only this, and make each change big and obvious: a long tail of straight dark brown hair (#4a3428) hangs from the top of its rump straight down behind its back legs to its heels, narrow at the root and wider at the end, one smooth sweep of hair, not bushy; its four legs are a little thicker; each hoof is a big flat-bottomed dark brown block, clearly wider than the leg above it. Nothing else changes, no dirt, no texture.

3. Wear, with the twist (`node tools/art3d/style.ts worn horse`; 2 drawn, the first kept as design.png: its scarf is one smooth band, where the second's knit ridges would trace as slivers).

4. Poses as sheets, three to a 16:9 image (two for wall and death) with design.png as the reference, each drawn twice and the better kept, cut apart with cut.py and scaled to the design's ink by sheet (A 1.6, B 2.2, C 2.2, D 1.7: the design's muzzle over each sheet's):
