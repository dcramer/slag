1. Design (4 drawn, drafts/design-1..4; design-1 kept: the biggest face, the candle, four feet):
A single original cartoon character for a tiny 2D platformer sprite, drawn once, large, full body, seen almost straight from the front, turned only slightly to the right, on a plain flat white background, no ground, no text, no labels, no frames, no other figures. Design it to read as a 24-pixel-tall game sprite: one silhouette idea, about three big shapes, the signature prop, the eyes, and almost nothing else. At most four flat colours plus the line colour; no fingers, no small accessories, no fine detail. Style: a thin hand-inked line in a dark of each colour, flat bright colour, no gradients, no glow, no shading. The character: a tall cartoon creature made from cardboard boxes, its face square to us and only a thin sliver of its right side showing. Colours: green cardboard (#5aa846) painted all over in a grid of big square pixel tiles in a lighter (#8cd06a) and a darker (#3c7a34) green, like low-resolution pixel camouflage, the face solid near-black (#1e2a1e), a white wax candle (#f2eee4) with a yellow flame (#f6c443). Its body is one tall upright box with crisp sharp square corners, twice as tall as it is wide. On top sits a big cube head exactly as wide as the body, no neck. Under the body four short square block feet, two at the front and two at the back peeking out at the sides, white background between them. No arms at all. The front of the head is a big black frowning face filling most of it: two large square black eyes near its top corners and, under the gap between them, a long black down-turned mouth, a tall block in the middle with a leg stepping down on each side. A short stubby birthday candle stands on top of the head, its small flame lit, wax dripping over the head's top edge. The top back corner of the head is dented in. No stripes, no bands, no tape.
