How design.png and the pose drawings were made (2026-11-05). Every image was 2 credits (no free images left this month): 27 images, 54 credits. Drafts are in drafts/, the pose sheets in sheets/ (sheet*-N the first drawings, stub*-N the same sheets with the candle burnt down), cut into flat/.

1. Design (`node tools/art3d/style.ts design creeper` as the brief first stood, the tall box; 4 drawn, drafts/design-1..4). All four came back as the original redrawn with a candle on. design-1 kept: four legs apart, the head no wider than the body.
   Design read, design-1 and design-3 as black silhouettes 24 px tall beside the sneak, miner, impostor, hopper, plumber and dragon designs, two fresh readers: "2: unsure. Thin tall slab with a small antenna/spike on top"; "2: unsure (low). Tall, narrow rectangle with a thin spike or antenna on top and small feet. Could be a block creature or a tall narrow robot." Both paired only the two candidates. In black his outline is a post.

   A second brief (a big cube head overhanging a thin slab on four splayed cube legs; 4 drawn, drafts/r2-1..4) read no better. With the face's four holes cut through the silhouette (as the game cuts `holes`), 50 px tall, one fresh reader: design-1 "Minecraft Creeper (low). A tall, thin rectangular body on short legs, with a frowning face cut out of the silhouette. A thin spike or antenna on top doesn't fit a Creeper; it reads more like a Bob-omb fuse"; r2-2 "unsure... Could be a Creeper or a Bob-omb mix". With only the frown cut it read as nothing. So the face is holes through the box, and design-1 the design.
