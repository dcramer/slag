1. Design, round 1 (`node tools/art3d/style.ts design assistant` as first written: a tuft "like a small fountain", the bell "at the front", 4 drawn, drafts/design-1..4). All four ours, a yellow dog girl in a vest and skirt. Traced black at 22 px they were a hooded blob: the tuft a one-pixel nub, the bell inside the head's outline, the ears one mass with the body. Not read.
   Round 2 (the brief as it stands in tools/art3d/style.ts: head half her height, the tuft half as tall again, the bell a third as wide as her head, past its outline, the ears flicked out; 4 drawn, drafts/r2-1..4). r2-4 kept: the tallest tuft, the ears hanging clear of the body.
   Design read, r2-1 and r2-4 as black silhouettes 22 px tall beside the puppy, mouse, top-hat girl, fighter and plumber designs, two fresh readers. r2-4: "unsure. A narrow, small body with a tuft or small hat on top"; "unsure, maybe the Animal Crossing dog secretary (low): slim upright body tapering at the bottom, small head with a tuft or ears on top". Both paired only the two candidates. (The same sheet read the plumber and the puppy "unsure" too: a design traced at 22 px is a harder test than the game's sheet.)

The same character exactly as in the reference image: the same design, proportions, colours, wear and tear, line and flat white background, in the same three-quarter view facing right. Draw her three times side by side in one row: three separate figures of the same size, with very wide white space between them, none touching or overlapping, each in its own pose, KEEP. Left: … Middle: … Right: … Nothing else changes: no ground, no shadow on the ground, no motion lines, no text, no other figures.
   - sheetA (walk-1, walk-2, idle2 reading the clipboard, hand on hip): A-1 and A-2 both good.
   - sheetB (run-1, run-2, air-1): B-1 kept; B-2's run-2 was another long stride.
   - sheetC (air-2 falling, cheer with the clipboard thrust out, cheer2 a twirl): C-1 kept; C-2's fall lost the clipboard.
   - sheetD, the far template ("The same character as in the reference image … drawn at the same size. Draw her twice side by side …", the wall slide side on, knocked out on her back with the clipboard dropped beside her): D-1 and D-2 (D-2 drew a wall line).
