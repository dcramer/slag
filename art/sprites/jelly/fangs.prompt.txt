The same character exactly as in the reference image: the same fluted green jelly dome, the same three red cherries inside it, the same colours, the same wear (the melting drip, the scooped-out top), the same line and flat white background. Change only this, and make it big and obvious: the dome becomes a rounded bell that flares out wider at its rim than anywhere above it, like a jellyfish's bell. Under it, the four fangs become four huge curved ivory hooks, each as long as the dome is tall, spread far apart like a parasite's mandibles: the two outer hooks sweep out wide to the sides past the rim and curve down with their tips curling in, the two inner hooks hang straight down and curl inwards at their tips, with wide open white space between every hook. Only four hooks, nothing else hangs under the rim: the doily is a thin flat disc stuck flat under it. Nothing else changes.
