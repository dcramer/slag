How design.png and the pose drawings were made (2026-11-06). Junior is no homage: the design is the reference (reference.png, https://junior.sentry.dev/junior-character.png) redrawn flat, every trait kept, no twist. Every image was 2 credits (no free images left). drafts/ holds the designs and edits, sheets/ the pose sheets, drawn/ each drawing as cut (and the design as drawn), flat/ and design.png each drawing ready to trace (prep.py).

1. Design, an edit of the reference (--ref reference.png --aspect 1:1, 2 drawn; drafts/design-1, design-2):
The same robot character exactly as in the reference image, redrawn as a simple flat cartoon sprite design for a tiny 2D platformer game: full body, three-quarter view facing right, on a plain flat white background, no ground, no text. Keep its whole design: a square yellow box head (#fdb819) with one huge round eye and a big toothy grin, a purple cap (#8a3f8c) worn backwards with its bill out behind, two zigzag antennae with ball tips springing up and out clear of the cap, round dark headphone ears; a pink hoodie (#ee4d9b); one long bendy yellow arm held out low ending in a big three-finger claw, the other hand holding up a phone; thin dark purple-grey legs (#4a4058) with yellow ankle bands, white socks and chunky purple roller skates (#6c3a78). Restyle only: fewer, bigger shapes, flat bright colour with one hard cel shadow, a thin dark hand-inked line, no gradients, no fine detail.
   design-1 kept: the reference's sleeveless hoodie, yellow arms and purple skates (design-2 gave the hoodie pink sleeves and grey skates).

4. Poses, three to a 16:9 image with drawn/design.png as the reference, 2 drawn each, cut with `build.ts cut junior SHEET NAME… --design`. Sheets A to C's prompt: "The same character exactly as in the reference image: the same design, proportions, colours, wear and tear, line and flat white background, in the same three-quarter view facing right. Draw him three times side by side in one row: three separate figures of the same size, with very wide white space between them, none touching, each in its own pose, always his square yellow box head with its one huge eye and big toothy grin, the cap on backwards with its bill out behind, the three thick zigzag antennae with ball tips standing clear of his head, the long bendy claw arm held out clear of his body, the phone in his other hand, the chunky roller skates. Left: … Middle: … Right: … No ground, no shadow, no motion lines, no text, no other figures." The poses are those in tools/art3d/flat/kinds/junior.ts. Sheet D (the wall slide and the knock-out) is two figures, "The same character as in the reference image … drawn at the same size", the kind's `far` poses.
   - sheetA-1: walk-1, walk-2. sheetA-2: idle2 (looking at the phone, tapping it; the claw tucked behind).
   - sheetB-2: run-1, air-1 (all three antennae sprung up). sheetB-1: run-2.
   - sheetC-1: air-2 (eye wide, claw flung out), cheer2 (a wink and a grin, the claw up, filming). sheetC-2: cheer (claw up snapping, phone up filming). Both sheets' right figures drew a five-finger hand for the claw: not used.
   - sheetD-1: wall (unwall.py whitened the drawn wall line first: sheetD-1-unwalled), death.

5. prep.py makes every drawing ready to trace: the drawn whites (eye, teeth, socks, phone screen) are tinted under the hole test's 228 so `holes` cuts only the background between his legs and arms, and the eye's white is tinted apart (`eyewhite`) so the face kit finds the pupil in it and paints both over.
