How design.png and the pose drawings were made (2026-11-04). Every image was 2 credits (no free images left): 26 images, 52 credits. The drafts are in drafts/; round 1's sheets in sheets/ and its cut poses in r1-flat/; what ships is sheets2/ cut into flat/.

1. Design (`node tools/art3d/style.ts design stinger` as the brief first stood: the horns "curve up and back to sharp points", "a long steel knitting needle"; 4 drawn, drafts/design-1..4). All four came back as the original redrawn, the needle held across her and pointing back. design-1 kept: the widest gap between the horns.
   Design read, design-1 and design-3 as black silhouettes 24 px tall beside the king, hedgehog, hopper, slayer, elf and dragon designs, two fresh readers: "Hornet (the horned bug with the needle, from the bug-kingdom metroidvania), medium-high. Two tall pointed horns on the head, a thin body, a flared cloak or skirt, and a long thin needle sticking out to the left"; "Hornet from Hollow Knight (medium). Tall two-pronged horned head on a small cloaked body, thin legs, and a long needle sticking out to the left." Each paired only the two candidates.

2. The twist and the wear, as one edit (`node tools/art3d/style.ts worn stinger`, --ref drafts/design-1.png, 3 drawn; drafts/worn-2 kept: the shortest yarn loop and the biggest patch):
The same character exactly as in the reference image: the same pose, design, proportions, colours, line and flat white background. Change only this, and make each change big and obvious: the cloak is an old striped dishcloth: two wide pale pink stripes (#e8a6a6) run across it near the hem, a big square patch of grey cloth (#a8a29a) is sewn on one side with big stitches, and the hem is frayed into a ragged fringe of loose threads; a strand of cream yarn (#e6dcc4) hangs from the round knob of the knitting needle in one short loose loop. The mask, its eye holes and the two horns stay exactly as they are. Nothing else changes, no dirt, no texture.

4. Round 2 (what ships): an edit of drafts/worn-2.png (3 drawn, drafts/r2-1..3; r2-3 kept as design.png: smooth crescents, r2-1 and r2-2 grew prongs on the horns):
The same character exactly as in the reference image: the same pose, mask, cloak, colours, wear, line and flat white background. Change only this, and make it big and obvious: the knitting needle is now much thicker and longer, a chunky steel knitting needle as thick as her leg and longer than she is tall, with a big round knob on its end, held level in her fist with its long sharp point sticking far out in front of her to the right, well past her mask, with white background all round it, and its knob just behind her fist, the yarn hanging from the knob; the two horns curve more, each a sharp crescent that sweeps up and hooks back at its tip. Nothing else changes, no dirt, no texture.
(The needle still points back past her, as in every drawing of her: it sticks out on both sides.)

Draw her three times side by side in one row: three separate figures of the same size, with very wide white space between them, none touching or overlapping, each in its own pose, KEEP. Left: … Middle: … Right: … Nothing else changes: no ground, no shadow on the ground, no motion lines, no text, no other figures.
   - sheetA (walk-1, walk-2, idle2 leaning on the needle as a cane): A-1 -> flat/walk-1, walk-2, idle2.
