How design.png and the pose drawings were made (2026-11-06). Every image was 2 credits (no free images left): 32 credits, 16 images. The drafts are in drafts/, the sheets in sheets/, the cut poses in flat/ (air-2's source in drawn/). No prompt was refused.

1. Design (`node tools/art3d/style.ts design mushroom`, 2 drawn, drafts/design-1..2). Both a white spotted dome cap over a small peach face, a red vest, white puffy trousers and a chequered flag on a broom handle, a bent wire under the rim. design-2 kept: its cap is far wider than him and sits lower, a clear dome with a thick rolled lip; design-1's was narrower, nearer a helmet, and it held the flag behind him.
   Design read, design-2 as a black silhouette at 22 px (shown at 2x) beside the plumber, assistant, mage, puffball, zapmouse, hopper and creeper designs, two fresh readers: "mushroom-cap retainer (the mushroom-head helper type), medium. A very large dome cap far wider than the small body, tiny legs, and a small object held out to the right" and "mushroom-cap retainer (medium): a big dome or cap head much wider than the small body, stubby legs and a small blob held out to the right". One said 4 and 5 (the puffball) "are both mostly big round masses, so they might be confused at this size; 4's dome cap over a small body sets it apart"; the other listed them as a possible pair for the same reason.

Left: … Middle: … Right: … Nothing else changes: no ground, no shadow on the ground, no motion lines, no text, no other figures.
   - sheetA (two scurrying steps, standing nervous with a hand on his belly): A-1 -> flat/walk-1, walk-2, idle2. A-2 lost the flag in the first and drew him brown shoes.
