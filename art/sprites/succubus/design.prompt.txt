The same character exactly as in the reference image: the same pose, design, proportions, colours, line and flat white background. Change only this, and make each change big and obvious: one of the felt back wings droops down limp off its bent wire hanger; a coat hanger's wire hook pokes up above her back shoulder; the front slipper is now a fuzzy bunny slipper with two long floppy ears and a round face; the back wing's felt has a big ragged tear. Her face, the two bat wings on her head and the gap between them and her hair stay exactly as they are. Nothing else changes, no dirt, no texture.

Draw her three times side by side in one row: three separate figures of the same size, with very wide white space between them, none touching or overlapping, each in its own pose, KEEP. Left: … Middle: … Right: … Nothing else changes: no ground, no shadow on the ground, no motion lines, no text, no other figures.
