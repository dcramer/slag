How design.png and the pose drawings were made (2026-11-04, Studio credits: 2 an image, 36 in all, 18 images).

1. Design (`node tools/art3d/style.ts design limbless`, with "its arms and legs a little clear of its body" and "the signature prop" taken out of the DESIGN preamble, since he has neither; 2 drawn). The first had the floating gloves and shoes and the tuft, but a bare skin body with a belt and no nose; the second wore a cape. The first was edited (2 drawn, the second picked, its knot behind the head and a white collar parting head from body):
The same character exactly as in the reference image: the same pose, the same floating white gloves and floating golden shoes with white space all round each, the same spiky brown hair tuft, the same line and flat white background. Change only this, and make each change big and obvious: his small round body is now a purple hooded top (#7b3fa6), the whole body purple, with a big round pale cream ring on the chest instead of the red button, and no belt; a bright red neckerchief (#c9473f) is tied round the bottom of his head where it meets the body, its knot and short points in front; he has a huge round peach nose sticking far out of the front of his face below the eyes, the biggest thing on his face. The gloves and shoes still float free of the body with wide white gaps all round them. Nothing else changes, no dirt, no texture.

2. Wear (`node tools/art3d/style.ts worn limbless`, that design as the reference; 2 drawn, the second picked: its gloves came back a pale cream, nearer bleached than the first's yellow, and its big shoe's toe curls up). Saved as drawn/design.png.

3. Poses, three to a 16:9 image with the design as the reference, cut apart with `build.ts cut … --design`. The prompt for each sheet:
The same character exactly as in the reference image: the same design, proportions, colours, wear and tear, line and flat white background, in the same three-quarter view facing right. Draw him three times side by side in one row: three separate full-body figures of the same size, with wide white space between them, none touching or overlapping, each in its own pose. He has no arms and no legs: in every figure his two gloves and two shoes float free of his body with white space all round each, nothing joining them to him, and each figure keeps its own gloves and shoes close round it, never straying toward the next figure. Left: <pose> Middle: <pose> Right: <pose> Nothing else changes: no ground, no shadow on the ground, no motion lines, no text, no other figures.
(The poses are each drawing's `poses` entry in tools/art3d/flat/kinds/limbless.ts. Sheet D, wall and death, two figures and the `far` opening.)

- sheets/sheetB.png → run-1, run-2 (air-1 redrawn in F)
- sheets/sheetC.png → none kept (air-2, cheer and cheer2 laid their gloves over the head or body)

Cut one figure at a time with the others painted white, since the shared cutter gave one figure's raised glove to its neighbour (cut.py joins a stray piece to the nearest figure's box, and the boxes grow as pieces join).

4. Spread (`python3 -I art/sprites/limbless/spread.py drawn/NAME.png flat/NAME.png`, and design.png the same way). The model draws the gloves and shoes about a world px off the body, which the cast's pen closes at play size: in the first build they read as attached. spread.py slides each floating piece straight out (the shoes down together, a glove sideways) until 90 image px of background, about 2 world px, part it from the body. drawn/ keeps the cut drawings it works from. run-2's front fist and the knocked-out pose's one glove touch the body in the drawing and stay where they are.
