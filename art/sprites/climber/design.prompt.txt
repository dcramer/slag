How design.png and the pose drawings were made (2026-11-06). Every image was 2 credits (no free images left): 16 images, 32 credits, no refusals. drafts/ holds the designs and wear edits, sheets/ the pose sheets, flat/ each drawing as cut and traced.

1. Design (2 drawn, drafts/design-1, design-2; a prompt of its own, under 900 characters; the brief is BRIEFS.climber):
A single original cartoon character for a tiny 2D platformer sprite, full body, seen side on in profile facing right, on a plain flat white background, no ground, no text, no other figures. Flat bright colours, a thin dark hand-inked line, one hard cel shadow, no gradients, no fine detail. A small determined girl leaning forward, one boot stepping ahead. Her signature, big and bold: a big messy mop of bright red hair (#c8382e), chin length, floating up and streaming back behind her head as if in a strong wind, as big as her head, with white background round its ragged tips. A puffy quilted parka to her hips made from a blue sleeping bag (#3f6fb5) with arms cut in, fat puffy bands (pale blue #a8c4e8), a red strawberry magnet on its pocket. A small grey lunchbox on a string as a backpack. Slim dark leggings, chunky brown boots, pale skin, dot eyes.
   design-1 kept: the hair streams back behind her as she leans into the step (design-2's blows forward, against her walk).

2. Wear (--ref drafts/design-1.png, 2 drawn; drafts/worn-1 kept as design.png: the stuffing out of the torn hem, the tape round the sleeve, the lunchbox's lid hanging open, the sole flapping; worn-2 put the stuffing on her leg):
The same character exactly as in the reference image: the same pose, design, proportions, colours, line and flat white background. Change only this, and make each change big and obvious: a big ragged tear in the parka's hem with a white tuft of stuffing poking out of it; a strip of grey tape (#9a9c98) wrapped round the near sleeve; the grey lunchbox's lid hanging open on one hinge; the back boot's sole flapping loose at the toe. Her face, the red hair's size and shape streaming back and the puffy parka stay exactly as they are. Nothing else changes, no dirt, no texture.
   Design read, worn-1 in colour (the parka recoloured green) at blind-sheet size beside the raider, archer, genie, top-hat girl, sim, fighter and slayer cut from the blind sheet, two fresh readers: "Madeline (Celeste), low: short red hair and a small walking sprite, but the green top doesn't match her usual coat" and "Madeline (Celeste) — low — short spiky red hair on a small walking figure; the green striped top and grey thing on the back don't match her". Neither paired her with the archer. Mocks with the hair 1.35 times as big, and the strawberry twice as big, read the same (low, three readers).

3. Poses, three to a 16:9 image with design.png as the reference, each drawn twice, cut apart with `build.ts cut climber SHEET NAME… --design`. Each sheet's shared words: "The same character exactly as in the reference image: the same design, proportions, colours, wear and tear, line and flat white background, in the same side view facing right. Draw her three times side by side in one row: three separate figures of the same size, with very wide white space between them, none touching or overlapping, each in its own pose, her big messy mop of red hair always floating up and streaming back behind her head with white background round its ragged tips, the puffy banded blue parka to her hips, the open grey lunchbox on a string on her back. Left: … Middle: … Right: … No ground, no shadow on the ground, no motion lines, no text, no other figures."
   - sheetA: walking briskly, leaning forward, mid-step / the legs passing under her / standing upright, mittens on her hips, determined. sheetA-1 -> walk-1, walk-2 (its right figure faced us); sheetA-2 -> idle2 (its right figure, side on, hands in her pockets).
   - sheetB: running, the near knee high / both boots off the ground, legs split / dashing up and forward in one straight diagonal line, arms reaching ahead, hair streaming straight back. sheetB-1 -> run-1, air-1; sheetB-2 -> run-2, air-2 (the dash again, arms level: her jump is a dash up and down).
   - sheetC (falling / cheering, a fist up / holding a big strawberry out): both drew her facing us. Not used.
   - sheetE, sheetC again with "every figure seen side on in profile facing right, like the reference, never facing us" and "in profile" on each pose: sheetE-2 -> cheer, cheer2 (its left figure, the fall, grew a white block behind her head); sheetE-1 faced us again.
   - sheetD (two figures: clinging to a wall just to her right, mittens high, boots braced, hair floating up / knocked out flat on her back): sheetD-1 -> wall, death; sheetD-2 drew the wall's line.
