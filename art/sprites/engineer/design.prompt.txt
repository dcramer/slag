How design.png was made (third round, 2026-11-03). Round one's spanner, carried up behind his head, and the toy turret read as "a capped figure with spiky bits"; round two's wide brim on a short, stocky man read as the plumber's rival. Each step below is an edit with the one before as the reference, starting from round two's worn design.

Four fresh designs from `node tools/art3d/style.ts design engineer` (then asking for a tall dome, a taller build and the spanner planted like a walking stick) came back lanky, three of them out of the colours; none was kept.

1. Dome and build (3 drawn; the first and third kept as references):
The same character as in the reference image: the same face, colours, clothes, line, three-quarter view and flat white background. Change only this, and make each change big and obvious: he is taller and leaner, with long legs, his head a third of his height; his hat is now a tall round deep yellow bowl dome with a short even rim all the way round, no wide brim; the dark goggle strap runs right round the dome as a band with the two lenses on its front; his jaw is a smooth clean-shaven square, no stubble; the spanner is now as long as he is tall and stands head-down on the floor beside his front boot, its big open C jaw on the floor, his gloved hand resting on top of its handle at chest height like a walking stick; nothing on his shoulder. Nothing else changes, no dirt, no texture.

2. Drag (3 drawn from each of those two; the first from the third picked: the others drew two spanners or kept him thin):
The same character as in the reference image: the same face, hat, goggles, colours, clothes, line, three-quarter view facing right and flat white background. Change only this, and make each change big and obvious: he is sturdier, with a broad square torso and thick arms and legs; he drags the spanner behind him: he grips the very end of its handle in the gloved hand down at his hip, and the spanner, much bigger now and as long as he is tall, slants down and back away behind him, its big head resting on the floor well behind his heels, its wide open C jaw facing up. Nothing else changes, no dirt, no texture.

3. Bigger spanner (3 drawn, the first picked; it came back double-ended, slanting up past his shoulder):
The same character exactly as in the reference image: the same pose, face, hat, goggles, colours, clothes, line and flat white background. Change only the spanner, and make it big and obvious: it is twice as big, as long as he is tall, its head as wide as his chest with a huge wide open C jaw; he still grips the very end of its handle in the gloved hand at his hip, and it slants down and back behind him, its head resting on the floor well behind his heels. There is only one spanner. Nothing else changes, no dirt, no texture.

4. Wear (`node tools/art3d/style.ts worn engineer`; 3 drawn, the first picked: the third cracked the dome's outline):
The same character exactly as in the reference image: the same pose, design, proportions, colours, line and flat white background. Change only this, and make each change big and obvious: a big dent is knocked into the side of the yellow dome; a chip is broken out of the bowl's rim at the back; the spanner's handle is snapped in the middle and bound with a big wad of grey tape; one trouser knee is torn open in a big ragged hole. Nothing else changes, no dirt, no texture.

5. Bigger hat, chunkier (3 drawn, the third picked: the biggest dome). A blind read of these poses still saw "tall and thin with a long curled tail": the spanner's lower jaw by his boots.
The same character as in the reference image: the same face, colours, clothes, wear, line, pose and flat white background, and the huge spanner exactly as it is, as long as he is tall, its big open jaw up beside his shoulder. Change only his proportions, and make each change big and obvious: his round yellow hard hat is much bigger, the dome as wide as his shoulders, with the dark goggle band round it; he is chunkier, a thick barrel torso, thick arms held close to his sides, thick legs close together and big chunky boots. Nothing else changes, no dirt, no texture.

6. One jaw, broader (3 drawn, the second picked: the handle stops just past his fist):
The same character exactly as in the reference image: the same face, hat, goggles, colours, clothes, wear, line, pose and flat white background. Change only this, and make each change big and obvious: the spanner has only one jaw, the big open C jaw up beside his shoulder; its other end is a plain rounded handle end that stops just below his gloved fist, so nothing of the spanner reaches down past his hip; and he is broader, his shoulders and chest much wider. Nothing else changes, no dirt, no texture.

7. Lantern jaw (3 drawn, the second picked: the widest, flattest-bottomed jaw):
