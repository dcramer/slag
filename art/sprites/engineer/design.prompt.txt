A further edit asking for the spanner lower and the handle ending in the fist changed nothing; one carrying it low at his side shrank it to a small double-ended spanner.

The same character as in the reference image: the same face, colours, clothes, line, three-quarter view and flat white background. Change only this, and make each change big and obvious: remove the orange toy turret from his belt, nothing on the belt; remove the long wooden broom handle; he now carries one huge plain grey adjustable spanner resting level on his shoulder, its handle lying flat along the top of his shoulder, gripped in the gloved hand right at the shoulder, and its big head sticking straight out behind his back at shoulder height, well below the bowl, its jaw a wide open C facing back; nothing sticks out in front of him; the yellow bowl's rim is wide and flat and sticks far out past his head all round like a hard hat brim; his body is boxier and squarer. Nothing else changes, no dirt, no texture.
