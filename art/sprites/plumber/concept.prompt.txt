Character model sheet for a 2D platformer video game, an original cartoon character design. Style: clean, light, hand-drawn line art with a thin hand-inked pen line that swells and tapers a little, in a dark warm brown rather than pure black. Flat cel colour: every area is one flat calm colour with one hard-edged darker cel shadow shape on the side away from the light, light from the upper left. No gradients, no texture, no soft shading, no glow, no rim light, no painterly rendering. Simple chunky readable shapes that still read when tiny. Plain flat white background, even light, no ground shadows, no text, no labels, no frames. Proportions pushed hard for a tiny game sprite: the head is half of the total height, the hands are big mitts and the feet big chunky boots, the arms and legs short and thick, the body a small round barrel. A chunky toy, never a realistic figure. Character: a short, stout foundry plumber with a huge round head, an egg-shaped round belly and short legs. A big round red nose that breaks his profile, a thick bushy dark-brown mustache under it, two big black oval eyes with a white glint, bushy brows, dark-brown hair at the sides. A soft red cap (#c9473f) with its bill sticking out front, a round riveted brass badge on the front stamped with a tiny wrench (no letter), and a pair of round dark welding goggles strapped around the crown of the cap, pushed up. Blue bib overalls (#3f62b4) fastened with two big steel hex bolts instead of buttons, a square patch on one knee, over a red shirt (#c9473f) with the sleeves rolled up. Huge tan leather work gauntlets with wide cuffs, huge brown steel-toe work boots with grey steel toecaps. The joke: an absurdly oversized rubber sink plunger, as long as he is tall, strapped diagonally across his back like a greatsword, its big dark rubber cup sticking up high above his head behind one shoulder and its copper-pipe handle poking out below the other hip. Cheerful, jaunty and proud of his plunger. Layout: one row of full-body views of the same character side by side: a front view, a side profile facing right, a back view showing the plunger strapped on, and a three-quarter view facing right.
