Character model sheet for a 2D platformer video game, an original cartoon character design. Style: clean, light, hand-drawn line art with a thin hand-inked pen line that swells and tapers a little, in a dark warm brown rather than pure black. Flat cel colour: every area is one flat calm colour with one hard-edged darker cel shadow shape on the side away from the light, light from the upper left. No gradients, no texture, no soft shading, no glow, no rim light, no painterly rendering. Simple chunky readable shapes that still read when tiny. Plain flat white background, even light, no ground shadows, no text, no labels, no frames. Proportions pushed hard for a tiny game sprite: the head is half of the total height, the hands are big mitts and the feet big chunky boots, the arms and legs short and thick, the body a small round barrel. A chunky toy, never a realistic figure. Character: a small, stubby, round blue hedgehog with a very big round head, a small round body and short stubby legs, like a chunky toy. Cobalt-blue quills (#2f5bd0) in four thick spikes swept straight back from the head, their tips scorched soot-black, and two short spikes on the back. A peach muzzle and belly (#f0c99c), two separate big round eyes with green irises and a white glint under heavy dark-blue brows, a small black nose, a cocky one-sided grin with one chipped tooth. A snug neckerchief of yellow-and-black hazard-striped cloth knotted at his throat. Big brown fingerless leather gloves. Huge red high-top work sneakers (#d8382f) with grey steel toecaps and a hazard-yellow strap across each. Confident and fast. Layout: one row of full-body views of the same character side by side: a front view, a side profile facing right, a back view showing the swept quills, and a three-quarter view facing right.
