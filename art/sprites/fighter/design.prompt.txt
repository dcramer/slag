How design.png and the pose drawings were made (2026-11-05/06), round 3 of the fighter: a new idea, her pose and outfit before her hair. Every image was 2 credits (no free images left): 27 images, 54 credits. Rounds 1 and 2 (big round head, buns as ears) are in r1/ and r2/; the first drawings of this round, with ribbons under the buns, in r3/. drafts/ holds this round's designs and edits, sheets/ its pose sheets, drawn/ each drawing as cut, flat/ and design.png each drawing ready to trace (prep.py).

1. Design (2 drawn, drafts/design-1, design-2; a prompt of its own, under 900 characters, the brief now in BRIEFS.fighter):
A single original cartoon character for a tiny 2D platformer sprite, full body, three-quarter view facing right, on a plain flat white background, no ground, no text, no other figures. Flat bright colours, a thin dark hand-inked line, one hard cel shadow, no gradients, no fine detail. A strong martial-artist woman in a very deep, wide fighting stance: knees bent and far apart, thick muscular thighs, white boots. A blue high-collared dress (#3f6fb5, pale blue trim #a8c4e8) with huge round puffed shoulders much wider than her head, its short skirt flaring out wide over her thighs, tied with a fluffy white dressing-gown cord. Front arm held forward in guard, back fist at her hip, a black spiked dog collar on each wrist. A small head, dark brown hair (#3a2a20), dot eyes; two small round hair buns low on the sides of her head at the temples, each in a white rolled-up sock with a short white ribbon hanging down.
   design-2 kept: the deepest stance and the widest skirt. Both put the buns on top of her head, not at the temples.

2. Wear and buns (--ref drafts/design-2.png, 2 drawn; drafts/worn-1 kept: both buns showing, the hem's notch, the sole flapping):
The same character exactly as in the reference image: the same pose, design, proportions, colours, line and flat white background. Change only this, and make each change big and obvious: her face turns a little more toward us so both of her round white sock-covered hair buns show, and both buns move down to the sides of her head at temple height, level with her eyes, one on each side, sticking out sideways clear of her head, each with a short white ribbon trailing down from it; the top of her head is plain round dark hair; the skirt's hem is torn into a big ragged notch at one side; the front boot's sole is flapping loose at the toe.
