How design.png and the pose drawings were made (2026-11-04). Every image was 2 credits (no free images left this month): 4 designs, 12 edits and 16 pose sheets, 32 images, 64 credits. The drafts are in drafts/; round 1's design and pose sheets (what ships) are design.png (drafts/low-1), sheets/ and flat/; round 2's are in r2/.

1. Design (`node tools/art3d/style.ts design fighter`, 4 drawn): drafts/design-1..4. 1, 2 and 4 drew the original's build, a small head over a tall body; 3 was the chunkiest, a toy with a big round head, big buns and dot eyes, and the most our own, so it went on. It had the spiked collar round her neck (a dark band under the face reads as a mouth at play size) and none on the wrists.

2. Wear (`node tools/art3d/style.ts worn fighter`, --ref drafts/design-3.png, 3 drawn; drafts/worn-2 kept: the clearest fist and both wrist collars). The edit also moves the collar to the wrists:
The same character exactly as in the reference image: the same pose, design, proportions, colours, line and flat white background. Change only this, and make each change big and obvious: the spiked collar is gone from her neck, the dress's high collar shows there; instead a thick black dog collar (#2e2a2a) with big steel spikes sticking out all round goes round each wrist; the toe end of each rolled-up sock has come loose and flops down from under its bun like a short ribbon; the dress's hem is torn into a big ragged notch at the side; the front boot's sole is flapping loose at the toe. Both buns stay big and round, one each side of her head. Nothing else changes, no dirt, no texture.

6. Round 1's poses (sheets/, flat/: what ships), three to a 16:9 image with design.png as the reference, 2 drawn each, cut with `build.ts cut fighter SHEET NAME… --design`. Each sheet of three opens: "The same character exactly as in the reference image: the same design, proportions, colours, wear and tear, line and flat white background, in the same three-quarter view facing right. Draw her three times side by side in one row: three separate figures of the same size, with very wide white space between them, none touching or overlapping, each in its own pose, her head always turned toward us so both round white sock-covered hair buns stand out, one on each side of the top of her head, the big puffed shoulders, the black spiked collars on both wrists, the thick strong legs under the short dress." It ends: "Nothing else changes: no ground, no shadow on the ground, no motion lines, no text, no other figures."
   - sheets/sheetA gives flat/walk-1, walk-2 and idle2 (all A-1; A-2 drew a blush). Left: walking to the right in a low fighter's crouch, mid-step, the near boot forward and the far boot back, both fists up in guard. Middle: walking to the right, the legs passing under her, one boot planted flat and the other lifted a little behind it, fists up in guard. Right: a ready stance, knees bent wide apart, bouncing on her toes, both fists raised in guard in front of her, her eyes open.
   - sheets/sheetB gives flat/run-1, run-2 and air-1 (all B-1; B-2 drew a blush and a frown). Left: running low and fast to the right, leaning forward, the near knee raised high in front and the far leg pushing off far behind, fists pumping. Middle: running to the right, mid-stride, both boots off the ground, the legs split wide apart front and back. Right: a flying kick in the air, her front leg kicked straight out forward level at hip height, the other knee tucked up under her, fists up, her eyes open.
   - sheets/sheetC gives flat/air-2, cheer and cheer2 (all C-1; C-2 drew big white eyes). Left: falling, both boots reaching straight down and apart, both arms out to the sides for balance, her eyes wide and her mouth open. Middle: a victory pose, standing tall, one fist on her hip and the other making a peace sign beside her cheek, one knee lifted, a big grin, her eyes open. Right: showing off, standing on one leg with the other leg kicked straight up high in front of her above her head, her arms out to the sides, a grin.
   - sheets/sheetD gives flat/wall (D-2 left: her palm flat on the wall) and death (D-1 right). The prompt: "The same character as in the reference image: the same design, proportions, colours, wear and tear, line and flat white background, drawn at the same size. Draw her twice side by side in one row: two separate figures with very wide white space between them, none touching or overlapping, both round white sock-covered hair buns showing in both. Left, seen from her side: she is sliding down a tall wall that is just to her right (do not draw the wall), one hand and both white boots braced flat against it, knees bent, her other fist out, her head turned to look back at us so both buns show, eyes open. Right, seen from the side: she has been knocked out and lies flat on her back on the floor, the whole body horizontal, her head on the left and her boots on the right, arms and legs splayed limp, her eyes shut. No ground, no shadow, no motion lines, no text, no other figures."
