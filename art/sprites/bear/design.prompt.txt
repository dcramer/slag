The same character exactly as in the reference image: the same pose, design, proportions, colours, line and flat white background. Change only this, and make each change big and obvious: a big coin slot is set in the middle of his belly with a large silver coin jammed half into it; the fur on his near forearm is torn open at the elbow joint, a grey metal rod showing through; the fur on his far knee is split open showing a grey metal joint; the bow tie hangs crooked; the top hat has a big dent in its side. The top hat stays exactly where it is, small and upright on top of his head with white gaps between it and both round ears, and the shower head and its short hose stay held up in front of his mouth. His face stays as it is. Nothing else changes, no dirt, no texture.
