How design.png and the pose drawings were made (2026-11-04). Every image was 2 credits (no free images left). The drafts are in drafts/, the sheets in sheets/.

1. Design (`node tools/art3d/style.ts design raider`, 4 drawn): drafts/design-3 picked, the chunkiest, the biggest head and the thickest braid in the widest curve, legs wide apart. All four drew the braid out behind her clear of the back, the orange water pistols with a tank on top, the satchel on a strap across her chest. Design read of design-3 as a black silhouette at 24 px beside the plumber, elf, slayer, worm, merc and hunter designs, two fresh readers: "Lara Croft (Tomb Raider), medium-high. A long braid or ponytail curving up and behind the head, a wide action stance and arms out" and "the braided tomb raider. Medium confidence. A long braid or ponytail arcs back and curls behind the head, the stance is wide and athletic, and both arms are held out as if aiming pistols". One said the worm "could" be her too ("something flowing off the head"), the other the elf ("a long shape trailing back from the head").

The same character exactly as in the reference image: the same pose, design, proportions, colours, line and flat white background. Change only this, and make each change big and obvious: the satchel's strap is torn and tied back together in a big knot, its flap hanging open; a strip of grey tape (#9a9c98) is wrapped round the near water pistol's cracked tank; one knee of her shorts' hem is torn into a ragged notch; one boot's sole is flapping loose at the toe. Her face, the braid and its shoelace bow stay as they are. Nothing else changes, no dirt, no texture.
