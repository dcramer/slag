How design.png and the pose drawings were made (2026-11-05). Every image was 2 credits (no free images left). The drafts are in drafts/, the sheets in sheets/, the cut poses in flat/. The first design run (8 credits) came back as another kind's images (a green block creature with a candle), not this prompt's; they were thrown away and the run repeated.

1. Design (`node tools/art3d/style.ts design tophat`, 4 drawn, drafts/design-1..4). All four a small girl under a tall plum top hat with a lilac band, a cape, a ponytail, a small cream umbrella: close to the original redrawn (a plain top hat with a band). design-4 kept: a tall hat on a wide brim, the umbrella held out level.
   Design read, design-1 and design-4 as black silhouettes 24 px tall beside the robot, elf, raider, plumber and stinger designs, two fresh readers. design-4: "Hat Kid (top-hat girl), medium-low. 2. The twist and the wear, as one edit (`node tools/art3d/style.ts worn tophat`, --ref drafts/design-4.png, 3 drawn; drafts/worn-3 kept as design.png: grey tin rims and the dent, the hair-tie band with its bow, the umbrella cream and level; worn-1 put the umbrella up behind the hat, where the two would read as one shape, and worn-2 painted the umbrella purple):
The same character exactly as in the reference image: the same pose, design, proportions, colours, line and flat white background. Change only this, and make each change big and obvious: the top hat is now clearly an old tin can: two raised ridged rings run round it near its top and its bottom, and a big dent is knocked into its side, its brim still wide and flat and the hat still straight up; its band is a fat lilac hair tie (#e0b0dc) knotted at the side; the umbrella grows huge: an open paper cocktail umbrella as wide as she is, on a long wooden toothpick stick, held out at arm's length in front of her, with white background all round it; the hem of the cape is torn into a big ragged notch; one striped sock has slipped down round her boot. Her face and the ponytail stay as they are. Nothing else changes, no dirt, no texture.

3. Poses, three to a 16:9 image with design.png as the reference, each drawn twice, cut with `build.ts cut tophat SHEET … --design`. Each prompt is the template below with the kind's KEEP (tools/art3d/flat/kinds/tophat.ts) and three poses:
The same character exactly as in the reference image: the same design, proportions, colours, wear and tear, line and flat white background, in the same three-quarter view facing right. Draw her three times side by side in one row: three separate figures of the same size, with very wide white space between them, none touching or overlapping, each in its own pose, KEEP. Left: … Middle: … Right: … Nothing else changes: no ground, no shadow on the ground, no motion lines, no text, no other figures.
   - sheetA (walk-1, walk-2, idle2 swinging the umbrella like a parasol): A-1 -> flat/walk-1, walk-2; A-2 -> flat/idle2 (the umbrella drawn furled, as a cane). A-1's idle2 drew its near pupil into the lid's line, and the build found one eye.
   - sheetB (two runs, a jump): B-2 -> flat/run-1, run-2, air-1. B-1 drew all three facing left.
   - sheetC (falling, the umbrella thrust up like a sword, a twirl): C-1 -> flat/cheer; C-2 -> flat/air-2, cheer2. C-1's twirl had swirl lines round it.
   - sheetD, the far template ("The same character as in the reference image … drawn at the same size. Draw her twice side by side …", the wall slide side on, knocked out on her back with the hat knocked off beside her): D-2 -> flat/wall, its drawn wall line whitened first (sheets/D-2w.png); D-1 -> flat/death.
