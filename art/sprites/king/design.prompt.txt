How design.png and the pose drawings were made (third round, 2026-11-04). The second round (r2/, its prompts in r2/design.prompt.txt) had the spiked shell with spikes all round and, at best, "maybe Bowser (low). Round body covered in spikes, could equally be a hedgehog or porcupine". This round draws him side on, carried by two shapes: a smooth shell dome with its rim and few big spikes along the top, and a big horned head out front with an open crocodile jaw. Every image was 2 credits (no free images left this month): 18 images, 36 credits. The candidates are in r3/.

1. Design, three-quarter view (`node tools/art3d/style.ts design king` with the dome-and-head brief in its first wording; 2 drawn, r3/design-*.png). Both came back a big smooth dome with four spikes on top and a horned, open-jawed head, but in silhouette the head sat inside one round mass with the shell.

2. Push (2 drawn from design-2, r3/push-*.png; neither kept, the horns longer but the head no further out):
The same character exactly as in the reference image: the same design, colours, line, three-quarter view facing right and flat white background. Change only this, and make each change big and obvious: his huge head is pushed far forward, out in front of his chest on a thick neck, his long blunt snout jutting well past his fists; his heavy jaw hangs wide open in a roar, the gap between his upper and lower jaw a big wide wedge with four big white fangs; his two cream horns are twice as long, sweeping up and back high above his head; the smooth green shell dome stays big and round on his back with its thick cream rim standing out as a lip, and only three big spikes along its top; his legs are planted wide apart, a big gap of white background between them; a thick tail sticks out behind him under the shell. Nothing else changes, no dirt, no texture.

3. Design, side on (2 drawn, r3/profile-*.png, the first kept: the snout far out in front with the jaw open to the outside, so the open jaw is a notch in the silhouette). The shared DESIGN preamble with its three-quarter view and its signature prop left out, then the brief now in style.ts:
A single original cartoon character for a tiny 2D platformer sprite, drawn once, large, full body, on a plain flat white background, no ground, no text, no labels, no frames, no other figures. Design it to read as a 24-pixel-tall game sprite: one silhouette idea, about three big shapes, the eyes, and almost nothing else. At most four flat colours plus the line colour; no shape smaller than a tenth of its height; no fingers, no small accessories, no fine detail, no texture. Style: a thin hand-inked line in a dark of each colour, flat bright colour with one hard-edged cel shadow shape on the side away from the light (light from the upper left), no gradients, no glow. The character: a hulking turtle king seen side on in profile, facing right, [... the rest of BRIEFS.king.design].

A quick blind read of the candidates' silhouettes beside five other kinds' designs (r3 profile-1, dome-2, design-2 and the second round's design, two fresh readers): profile-1 "Spiky-shelled turtle king (Bowser parody): medium. Spiked shell, horned head, bulky body, snout on the right" and "spiked turtle king (Bowser parody) - medium - wide, heavy body with a spiky shell outline on the back and horn points on top"; design-2 low and unsure ("round, chunky blob with bumps on top").

4. Dome and crown (2 drawn from profile-1, r3/dome-*.png, the first kept: the crown on and the head as it was; the second gave the shell two huge spikes like ears):
The same character exactly as in the reference image: the same face, open crocodile jaw, horns, mane, colours, line, side-on view facing right and flat white background. Change only this, and make each change big and obvious: his head sits further forward on a thick neck, clear of the shell, with a dip between the back of his head and the top of the shell; the shell is a big smooth green dome with only two huge cream cone spikes on it, high on its back, pointing up and back, nothing else breaking its smooth curve; its thick cream rim sticks far out round its lower edge like the brim of a bowl; a small pale-blue paper party crown (#9cc8e8) with three points sits low between his horns; his legs are planted wide apart with a gap of white background between them. Nothing else changes, no dirt, no texture.

5. Wear (`node tools/art3d/style.ts worn king` on dome-1; 2 drawn, r3/worn-*.png, the first kept as design.png: its bent spike is the clearer):
The same character exactly as in the reference image: the same pose, design, proportions, colours, line and flat white background. Change only this, and make each change big and obvious: a big square grey riveted patch plate (#9aa0a8) is bolted flat onto the middle of the green shell; a big chunk is broken out of the shell's cream rim near the bottom; the pale-blue paper crown sits crooked, one of its points torn and folded down; one of the shell's spikes is bent over sideways. Nothing else changes, no dirt, no texture.

6. Poses, three to a 16:9 image with design.png as the reference, 2 drawn each (r3/sheet*-*.png), cut apart by connected shapes and each scaled x1.55 to the design's drawn size. Then the eye in every drawing (and design.png; unpainted it is r3/worn-1.png) was inked solid: the eye's white closed in by the ink and its pupil, the topmost white patch of the head, since the fangs are white too and stay bone.
