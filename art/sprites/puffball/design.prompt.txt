How design.png and the pose drawings were made (the second twist, 2026-11-06). The owner found the first drawing too like the original: its vinyl toy's seam and valve had been cut as misreads, leaving the original's own ball and tall oval eyes. This round keeps the ball, the big dark feet and the sweet face, and gives it stuck-on googly eyes. Every image was 2 credits.

1. Design (`node tools/art3d/style.ts design puffball` with the keyring brief; 4 drawn, the second kept: the biggest googly eyes, flat pupils, the biggest ring):
A single original cartoon character for a tiny 2D platformer sprite, drawn once, large, full body, in a three-quarter view facing right, its arms and legs a little clear of its body, on a plain flat white background, no ground, no text, no labels, no frames, no other figures. Design it to read as a 24-pixel-tall game sprite: one silhouette idea, about three big shapes, the signature prop, the eyes, and almost nothing else. At most four flat colours plus the line colour; no shape smaller than a tenth of its height; no fingers, no small accessories, no fine detail, no texture. Style: a thin hand-inked line in a dark of each colour, flat bright colour with one hard-edged cel shadow shape on the side away from the light (light from the upper left), no gradients, no glow. The character: a cheap claw-machine prize keychain of a round pink puffball (#f2a0bc), a near-perfect ball for a body with no neck or legs, two stubby round arms, two big red oval feet (#c9473f), pink blush, a small smile. A big steel keyring loop (#b9bcbf), a thick round ring as wide as a third of the ball, stands straight up out of the top of its head on a short metal eyelet screwed into its crown. Its eyes are two big stuck-on googly craft eyes, side by side and almost touching: round flat white plastic discs with a dark rim, each with a loose round black pupil, the pupils rolled two different ways, one slid down to the bottom of its disc and the other up into the top corner.

4. Poses, with design.png as the reference, 2 drawn each, cut with `build.ts cut puffball SHEET NAME… --design`. Then each drawing's pupils were painted out of their discs and a dot set in each disc's middle (eyes.py; idle2's dots level, its head tipped back), and the knocked-out drawing's shut discs painted white round an ink cross by hand. The unpainted drawings are in drawn/.
