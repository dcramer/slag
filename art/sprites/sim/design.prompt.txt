How design.png and the pose drawings were made (2026-11-05). Every image was 2 credits (no free images left): 14 images, 28 credits. The drafts are in drafts/, the sheets in sheets/, each drawing as cut in drawn/.

1. Design (`node tools/art3d/style.ts design sim`, --count 2, twice).
   drafts/design-1, design-2: the first brief, "an ordinary slim cartoon woman". Both drew a realistic tall slim woman, a small head on long legs: traced black at 28 px she was a stick under a dot. Not picked.
   drafts/chunky-1, chunky-2: the brief as it is in style.ts ("a chunky little cartoon woman like a toy figure, her head a third of her height", the bob "round and wider than her face", the dress "flaring out wide into a big bell"). chunky-2 picked: the wave at shoulder height, the slimmer double point.

2. Wear (`node tools/art3d/style.ts worn sim`, --ref drafts/chunky-2.png, 2 drawn): drafts/worn-1 kept as drawn/design.png: the taped crack, the hem's notch, the back shoe flat with its heel gone. worn-2 tipped the diamond into a lozenge and frayed the hem into thin threads.
The same character exactly as in the reference image: the same pose, design, proportions, colours, line and flat white background. Change only this, and make each change big and obvious: the green diamond is cracked across its middle and a strip of grey tape (#9a9c98) is stuck over the crack, and it hangs crooked, tilted to one side, still floating clear above her head with white background under it; the hem of the dress is torn into a big ragged notch at the side; the heel of her back shoe has snapped off, that shoe now flat. Her face, the bob and the gap under the diamond stay as they are. Nothing else changes, no dirt, no texture.

3. The diamond. The wear edit's tape, laid across the middle, hid the diamond's two side points (rotated, it was a lozenge), so no drawn diamond ships: gem.py draws one (diamond.png), a long double point with its lit facet, a ridge and a short strip of tape on its upper far facet, and float.py paints each drawing's own diamond out and hangs that one back over her head (floats.txt: the gap, the tilt and how far behind her it lags, a drawing each). design.png is drawn/design.png floated.
   Design read, design.png black at 28 px beside the plumber, raider, mage, fighter, top-hat girl and elf designs, two fresh readers: "a Sims character, medium-high. A diamond floating separately above the head, a small figure in a skirt or dress with a round head" and "Sims-style person with the floating diamond (high). A diamond floats clearly above the head, the figure is small with a dress or skirt, and the stance is neutral and upright". Neither paired her.
