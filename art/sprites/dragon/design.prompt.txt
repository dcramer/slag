2. The twist (2 drawn from design-2, the second kept: the first's jar had a soft glow round it, which would trace as a blotch):
The same character exactly as in the reference image: the same pose, design, proportions, colours, line, side view facing right and flat white background. Change only this, and make each change big and obvious: the tail ends in a big flat pale bone arrowhead tip (#ecdcb8), a sharp triangle pointing back like the head of an arrow, with two barbs, wider than the tail; the two wings become small stubby wings made of a folding umbrella's cloth in light lilac (#c4a2ea): each wing is three thin bent black metal umbrella spokes fanning out from the shoulder, each spoke ending in a small round black knob tip, with the cloth stretched between them in shallow scallops; a small round glass jam jar with a screw lid, a pale yellow firefly glowing inside it, hangs on a short string from the tail just in front of the arrowhead. Nothing else changes, no dirt, no texture.
