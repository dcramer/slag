Character model sheet for a 2D platformer video game, an original cartoon character design. Style: clean, light, hand-drawn line art with a thin hand-inked pen line that swells and tapers a little, in a dark warm brown rather than pure black. Flat cel colour: every area is one flat calm colour with one hard-edged darker cel shadow shape on the side away from the light, light from the upper left. No gradients, no texture, no soft shading, no glow, no rim light, no painterly rendering. Simple chunky readable shapes that still read when tiny. Plain flat white background, even light, no ground shadows, no text, no labels, no frames. Proportions pushed hard for a tiny game sprite: the head is half of the total height, the hands are big mitts and the feet big chunky boots, the arms and legs short and thick, the body a small round barrel. A chunky toy, never a realistic figure. Character: a slim young elf hero, never stout. A blond fringe (#e6c25a), long pointed ears, blue eyes. A green tunic (#3f7a34) flared at the hem, a wide brown belt, cream sleeves and leggings, brown gloves and boots (#6b4a35). On his back as a shield, a dented round steel dustbin lid (#a8adb4) with its handle; a black iron fireplace poker (#4a4a52) with a hooked tip in place of a sword, its loop handle over his shoulder. No sword, no bow.
