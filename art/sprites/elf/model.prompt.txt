A clean full-body 3D character model of an original cartoon character for a stylised platformer game, like a smooth painted vinyl toy figure. Chunky simple sculpted forms with clear separations between parts, every part one flat matte colour, no outlines, no painted shadows, no texture, no gloss. Lit flat and even from the front. One figure only, the whole body in frame from cap to shoes, centred. Plain light grey background, no ground shadow, no text. Proportions pushed hard for a tiny game sprite: the head is half of the total height, the hands are big mitts and the feet big chunky boots, the arms and legs short and thick, the body a small round barrel. A chunky toy, never a realistic figure. The same character as the reference sheet, with exactly its design, proportions and colours. Character: a slim young elf hero, never stout. A blond fringe (#e6c25a), long pointed ears, blue eyes. His cap: a very long floppy green woolly sock (#3f7a34), its toe end flopping back and hanging down his back to his waist, a light green darn (#5a9a4a) on its heel. A green tunic (#3f7a34) flared at the hem, a wide brown belt, cream sleeves and leggings, brown gloves and boots (#6b4a35). On his back as a shield, a dented round steel dustbin lid (#a8adb4) with its handle; a black iron fireplace poker (#4a4a52) with a hooked tip in place of a sword, its loop handle over his shoulder. No sword, no bow. Front view, standing in a T-pose: both arms straight out to the sides at shoulder height, hands open, fingers together, legs straight and a little apart, feet flat. The sock cap flops straight back and down behind his head to the nape, clear of the arms. The dustbin lid sits flat on his back and the poker lies behind it, both clear of the arms and head.
