How design.png and the pose drawings were made (2026-11-06). Every image was 2 Studio credits (no free images left). drafts/ holds the designs and edits, sheets/ the pose sheets, flat/ each drawing as cut (`build.ts cut mech SHEET NAME… --design`).

1. Design, round 1 (2 drawn, drafts/design-1, design-2; a prompt of its own, under 900 characters):
A single original cartoon character for a tiny 2D platformer sprite, full body, three-quarter view facing right, plain flat white background, no ground, no text. Flat bright colours, a thin dark hand-inked line, one hard cel shadow, no gradients, no fine detail. A squat round walking washing machine, much wider than tall, its shell pink (#e86aa8) with pale pink panels (#f6b8d4), a big round porthole door on its front with a thick grey metal rim and colourful laundry tumbling behind the glass. It stands on two thick grey metal bird legs that bend backward at the knee, with big three-toed feet. Two short thick grey ribbed dryer-duct tubes jut straight forward from its sides like stubby arms. A white bunny-shaped iron-on patch on its side. Over its top edge peeks a small young woman's head: brown hair, big white headphones, dot eyes.
   design-1 the better (design-2 faced left and was a box), but black at 22 px it was a round blob on stubs: its ducts curled in at its sides, inside the outline.

3. Shape edit (--ref drafts/design2-2.png, 2 drawn; drafts/edit1-1 kept, the cleaner legs):
The same character exactly as in the reference image: the same pose, design, colours, line and flat white background. Change only this, and make each change big and obvious: the two legs become thick chunky armoured grey mech legs, three times as thick, each knee bending sharply backward like a bird's, with big wide flat feet; the young woman's head peeking over the top is twice as big, with long brown hair falling behind it and big round white headphone cups on both sides of her head; the two hoses running back across the body's side are gone, only the two straight grey duct cannons sticking forward stay, a little thicker.
