How design.png and the pose drawings were made (2026-11-06). Every image was 2 credits (no free images left). drafts/ holds the designs and wear edits, sheets/ the pose sheets, drawn/ each drawing as cut (and the design as picked), flat/ each drawing ready to trace (prep.py).

1. Design (2 drawn, drafts/design-1, design-2; a prompt of its own, under 900 characters; the brief is BRIEFS.skeleton):
A single original cartoon character for a tiny 2D platformer sprite, full body, three-quarter view facing right, plain flat white background, no ground, no text. Flat bright colours, thin dark ink line, one hard cel shadow, no gradients, no fine detail. A short stocky lazy cartoon skeleton: a huge round bone-white plastic skull as wide as his shoulders, a fixed wide toothy grin, two big black eye sockets, the near one with a pale cyan glowing pupil, a small plastic hanging loop sticking up off the top of the skull. A baggy blue hoodie two sizes too big, hem at his knees, a thick grey fur-trimmed hood down round his neck, both hands deep in the pockets, a red ketchup squeeze bottle sticking up out of the side pocket. Dark grey shorts, thin bone legs with rivets at the knees, fluffy pale grey slippers.
   design-2 kept: the bigger loop with its eye open, the bottle standing proud of the pocket. Both lit the far socket, not the near one.
   Design read, design-2 in colour at 24 px with its hoodie turned green, among six cast designs (the plumber, the cuptoon, the limbless hero, the puffball, the boxer, the engineer), one fresh reader: "Sans (Undertale) — medium — round skull head on a short body in a hoodie, but the hoodie is green instead of blue". Not paired.

2. Wear and the twist pushed (--ref drafts/design-2.png, 2 drawn; drafts/worn-1-2048 kept, resized to 1024 as drawn/design.png: the ragged hem changes the outline, the seam runs over the skull; worn-2 tore a dark hole in the belly):
The same character exactly as in the reference image: the same pose, design, proportions, colours, line and flat white background. Change only this, and make each change big and obvious: a raised plastic moulding seam line runs over the top of the skull from the hanging loop down the back of the skull, like a cheap plastic toy's; the blue hoodie's hem has a big ragged tear on one side; the near slipper's sole has come loose at the toe and flaps open; the red ketchup bottle's cap is snapped open and bent; the round rivet at the near knee is missing, leaving a dark hole. The skull, the grin, the eye sockets, the glowing pupil, the hanging loop, the grey fur hood and the hands in the pockets stay exactly as they are. Nothing else changes, no dirt, no texture.

3. Poses, three to an image with design.png as the reference, each drawn twice, cut apart with `build.ts cut skeleton SHEET NAME… --design`. Each sheet: "The same character exactly as in the reference image: the same design, proportions, colours, wear and tear, line and flat white background, in the same three-quarter view facing right. Draw him three times side by side in one row: three separate figures of the same size, with very wide white space between them, none touching or overlapping, each in its own pose, KEEP. Left: … Middle: … Right: … Nothing else changes: no ground, no shadow, no motion lines, no text, no other figures."
   KEEP: "every one keeping the huge round grinning skull with its black sockets and one cyan pupil, the hanging loop on top, the grey fur hood, the baggy blue hoodie (with both hands in its pockets) and the red ketchup bottle in its side pocket".
   - sheetA (two lazy walks, leaning back with the ketchup held out): A-1 -> drawn/walk-1, idle2; A-2 -> drawn/walk-2.
   - sheetB (two jogs, a lazy hop with a wink): B-2 -> drawn/run-1, run-2, air-1. Both hops winked; air-1's shut eye is painted open (prep.py).
   - sheetC (falling with the arms flung out, a thumbs up, a ketchup toast; "his eye sockets open in every one"): C-1 -> drawn/air-2, cheer; C-2 -> drawn/cheer2 (the bottle clear of the skull).
   - sheetD, the far template ("The same character as in the reference image … drawn at the same size. Draw him twice side by side …": the wall slide, knocked out on his back): D-2 with its drawn wall line whitened (bear/wallline.py, columns 666-677, sheets/sheetD-2w.png) -> drawn/wall, death. D-1 drew him sitting back, not on a wall.

4. Made ready (prep.py, from drawn/ into flat/ and design.png; it is the record of what ships): air-1's wink painted open; the sockets filled solid, the teeth closed into one band in a placeholder colour (the `teeth` role) with a dark rim round them (`lip`), a nose painted afresh in its own colour (`nose`; in ink it ran into a socket or the seam and the face kit took it for a speck), the skull's gloss painted bone (sockets.py); the crown above the sockets redrawn as a round dome with the moulding seam over it (dome.py); the skull 1.1 times bigger about its chin (head.py).

   - Round 2, the loop 1.8 times bigger, bone whiter, body 23 px: "unsure: white skull- or ghost-like head on a yellow-green body"; "unsure — pale ghost-like head on a stubby body".

   - Round 4, the crown redrawn round, the loop a small ring sat on it: "unsure — white egg-shaped head, yellow body"; "unsure — white egg- or skull-shaped head, yellow-green sweater".
   - Round 5, the same with no loop: "Sans (Undertale) — medium — round skull head, hoodie"; "Sans (Undertale) — medium — big round skull, hoodie, short body". Neither paired him.
   - Round 6, a bigger ring on a narrow neck above the round crown: "unsure: white round head with an antenna, yellow body"; "unsure: white skull-like head on a yellow-green body".
   So the loop is cut (dome.py's RING), and round 5's drawings are what ships.
