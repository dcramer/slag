How design.png and the pose drawings were made (2026-11-04, Studio credits: 2 an image, 16 in all).

1. Design (`node tools/art3d/style.ts design ape`; 2 drawn, the first picked): both were the same hunched gorilla with knuckles on the floor; the first wore the tie more crooked and its taped fists read cleaner, the second had fur fringes at the wrists.

2. Wear (`node tools/art3d/style.ts worn ape`, the first design as the reference; 2 drawn, the first picked): its tie stays on the chest with a ragged tip, where the second's blew sideways over the arm and stopped reading as a tie. Its peeled banana stands up over the crown.

3. Poses, three to a 16:9 image with the design as the reference, cut apart at the white between the figures (each keeps its drawn scale; `body` sizes every pose alike). The prompt for each sheet:
The same character exactly as in the reference image: the same design, proportions, colours, wear and tear, line and flat white background, in the same three-quarter view facing right. Draw him three times side by side in one row: three separate full-body figures of the same size, with wide white space between them, none touching or overlapping, each in its own pose. Left: <pose> Middle: <pose> Right: <pose> Nothing else changes: no ground, no shadow on the ground, no motion lines, no text, no other figures.
(The poses are each drawing's `poses` entry in tools/art3d/flat/kinds/ape.ts. For sheetD, wall and death, two figures and the `far` opening: "The same character as in the reference image: the same design, proportions, colours, wear and tear, line and flat white background, drawn at the same size.")
