How design.png and the pose drawings were made (2026-11-04, the redraw). Every image was 2 credits (no free images left): 23 images, 46 credits. The first version, its drafts, sheets and prompts, is in r1/ (r1/design.prompt.txt; its paths are relative to r1/). A full-cast blind read took it for "Princess Peach or Zelda, low. Slim, with long hair and a dress or gown silhouette": the long coat read as a gown, the hair as a princess's.

1. Design (`node tools/art3d/style.ts design slayer`, the brief as it is now; drafts/design-1..4). All four came back as crouched brutes with no coat, no bottle cap and no cat's eyes, the blade across the legs; none kept.

2. The build, as an edit of the first version's design (r1/drafts/one-3.png, which has the face, eyes, cap and coat), 3 drawn, drafts/hero-3 kept:
The same character as in the reference image: the same face with its yellow cat eyes and scar, the same white hair colour, the same green coat colour, the same crimped bottle cap on his chest, the same sword hilt and umbrella crook over his shoulder, the same line and flat white background, in the same three-quarter view facing right. Change only this, and make each change big and obvious: he is now a broad-shouldered heroic fighter with a thick chest, standing in a wide stance with his legs far apart and his knees a little bent; his coat is now short, only to mid-thigh, open and split up the front, so below it his two legs show clearly apart in dark grey trousers and tall brown boots; his white hair is pulled back tight against his head into a short tail at the nape, nothing hanging past his shoulders; in his front hand he holds a long straight silver sword low in a guard, its long blade pointing forward and down beside his front leg, the tip ahead of his front boot, clear of his body and legs. The two hilts still stick up together above his back shoulder, crossing in an X, well clear of his head. No dirt, no texture.

3. Two edits to bring the blade out in front of him clear of his legs (drafts/guard-*, from hero-3; drafts/front-*, from guard-3): the model kept it across his legs both times. In silhouette it still shows as a long diagonal, its pommel out behind and its tip by the front boot.

Design read of guard-3 beside six cast designs, two fresh readers: "unsure, a hooded archer or ranger, low. Several thin sticks jut up behind the head (a quiver? one has a curl at the tip), the figure is narrow and hooded, there's a small item at the lower left hand, and it stands in a wide stance"; "elf-like sword hero with a sword on his back (low-medium). Several thin prongs stick up over the left shoulder like a hilt and crossguard or a quiver of arrows, hooded or capped head", pairing him with the elf.

4. Bigger hilts and the tail as a tuft (drafts/hilts-*, from guard-3; hilts-2 kept, the only one with both over one shoulder):
The same character exactly as in the reference image: the same design, face, coat, colours, stance, sword in his hand, line and flat white background. Change only this, and make each change big and obvious: the two hilts over his shoulder are now much bigger and thicker and stick up much higher, far above his head: the steel sword's hilt has a wide straight cross-guard as wide as his head, a big bold cross shape with a thick grip and a round pommel, and beside it the umbrella's crook is a big thick hooked J, both tilted up and back and crossing, with white background between them and his head; his short white hair tail now sticks out stiffly backwards from the nape of his neck like a short brush, a clear bump behind his head. No dirt, no texture.
Design read of hilts-2, two fresh readers: "Unsure. Probably a hooded robed wizard. Confidence: low. Clues: bulky cloaked body, a staff with a curled hook at the top held up at the left, and a robe hem flaring into points"; "unsure. A hooded or robed round body with a flared hem, a staff or pitchfork with a hooked, curly top held up at the left ... a generic wizard or reaper." No pairing.

5. A straight umbrella handle and a straight coat (drafts/straight-*, from hilts-2; straight-1 kept as design.png: the umbrella's handle a wooden knob, so the twist still shows in colour):
The same character exactly as in the reference image: the same design, face, colours, stance, sword in his hand, steel sword hilt over his shoulder, line and flat white background. Change only this, and make each change big and obvious: the umbrella on his back now has a straight thick handle with a round knob on its end instead of a hooked crook, so its handle sticks up beside the sword's hilt like a second sword hilt, the two straight hilts side by side above his back shoulder, its furled black cloth still showing below; his coat now hangs straight and close to his body down to mid-thigh, its hem cut level and square, never flaring out, his two legs clearly apart below it. No dirt, no texture.
Design read of straight-1, two fresh readers: "monster hunter with swords on the back (Witcher/Geralt parody), low. Two or three thin prongs (sword hilts or a quiver) stick up over the left shoulder, a hooded or cloaked bulky body leans forward, and a long staff or blade runs diagonally down to the lower left"; "Unsure. A three-pronged shape sticks up and back from the head (a trident, fork or plume), on a bulky body with a thin stick angling down to the lower left." No pairing.
No wear edit: the budget went to the outline. The worn tells in this drawing are the wear slot's marks only.
