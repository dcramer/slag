How design.png and the pose drawings were made (2026-11-04). Every image was 2 credits (no free images left): 45 images, 90 credits. The drafts are in drafts/; the sheets of the three blind-read rounds in sheets/, sheets2/ and sheets3/ (what ships).

1. Design, three rounds of four (`node tools/art3d/style.ts design slayer`, as the brief stood each time):
   - drafts/design-1..4: a long coat, a small head and thin hilts. At 24 px each was a plain pillar in silhouette.
   - drafts/designB-1..4: a big head, the ponytail sticking out behind. B-1 and B-3 read well, but the ponytail streaming back was the elf's flopping sock cap in outline and the sword hilt merged into the hair; B-2 and B-4 drew four handles.
   - drafts/designC-1..4 (the brief as it is now): C-3 picked, a long flared coat with a sword's cross and an umbrella's crook standing up one each side of the head. C-1 drew four handles, C-2 a giant sword in hand (the merc's), C-4 a short tunic.
   Design read of C-3 as a black silhouette at the blind sheet's size, beside the plumber, elf, hunter, merc, sneak and engineer designs, two fresh readers: "two-sword monster hunter (Witcher-style), medium. Two hilts show over the shoulders, one of them curved like a hook or crook, and the figure is lean in a long coat or robe that reaches the ankles"; "the white-haired monster slayer with two swords on his back, medium. A slim upright figure, two hilts above the shoulders in an X (one hooked), and a long coat down to the ankles." Neither paired it with another figure.
