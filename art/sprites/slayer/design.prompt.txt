1. Design, no reference, 2 + 2 drawn (drafts/d1-side-1..2, d1-tq-1..2). All four a heavy-set white-haired hunter in a wide low crouch, the sword in both hands. d1-side-1 kept: the blade level and straight out ahead of him.
A single original cartoon character for a tiny 2D platformer sprite, full body, seen from the side facing right, on a plain flat white background, no ground, no text, no other figures. Thin hand-inked line, flat colours, one hard-edged cel shadow, no texture. A heavy-set, broad-shouldered monster hunter crouching very low in a wide side-on fencing guard, knees deeply bent, front boot far forward and back boot far back. Both brown-gloved hands grip a long straight silver longsword held low at his hip, its broad blade pointing straight forward and level, reaching far out ahead of him, as long as his whole body. Two straight hilts with wide cross-guards stand up high side by side above his back shoulder, clear of his head: a steel sword's, and a furled black umbrella's straight wooden handle. A short tight green leather jacket ending at the belt, nothing that flares, dark trousers, tall brown boots. White hair tied back tight, yellow cat eyes, a grim frown, a round silver bottle cap on his chest.
   (d1-tq: the same in "a three-quarter view", "a grim, heavy-set brawler", the blade "clear of his legs"; both drew it slanting down.)
   Design read of d1-side-1 as a black silhouette 52 px tall beside nine cast designs, one fresh reader: "unsure, low. Maybe Geralt-style witcher, maybe a Doom-marine type. Bulky armored body in a wide, low crouch, a long blade held level to the right, and two thin spikes behind the head that could be sword hilts." Unpaired.

2. Bigger hilts (drafts/d2-hilts-1..2, --ref d1-side-1; d2-hilts-2 kept, both crossbars clear):
The same character exactly as in the reference image: the same face, yellow cat eyes, white hair, green jacket, bottle cap, colours, low wide crouch, the sword held level in both hands pointing straight forward, line and flat white background. Change only this, and make each change big and obvious: the two hilts over his back shoulder now stand up much taller and thicker, straight up side by side with white background between them and between them and his head, reaching far above his head: the steel sword's hilt with a wide straight cross-guard bar and a round pommel, and beside it the furled black umbrella's straight wooden handle with its own short wooden crossbar, so both read as two big sword hilts; the long sword in his hands has a broader, thicker blade, still level and as long as his body. No dirt, no texture.
   Design read of d2-hilts-2, a second fresh reader: "Unsure, maybe the horned shovel knight or a big-sword swordsman (low). Two thin spikes stick up from the head (horns, or hilts on the back), the stance is a wide low crouch, and a long straight blade is thrust forward." Unpaired: in black his head sat inside the shoulders' mound, so the hilts rose out of it like horns.
