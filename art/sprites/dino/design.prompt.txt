How design.png and the pose drawings were made (2026-11-04), on paid Studio credits (2 an image).

1. Design (`node tools/art3d/style.ts design dino`; 2 drawn, the second picked): both drew the original's head and
body, the twist as props; the second had the clearer bicycle seat on its chrome stem, its arms clear of the body.

2. Wear (`node tools/art3d/style.ts worn dino`; 3 drawn from the second design, the second picked: the black seat
torn open in a flap with its foam showing, the bent stem, a snapped head spike, a welly folded down, the bag torn).

3. Tail and seat, an edit of the worn design (3 drawn, the third picked). The first poses' blind read was split:
one reader named the dino, the other saw "a capped hero", the head and snout reading as a cap and bill, the short
tail lost in the body and the seat up by the head:
The same character exactly as in the reference image: the same pose, face, design, proportions, colours, wear and tear, line and flat white background. Change only this, and make each change big and obvious: its tail is much longer and thicker, a big green tail sticking straight out behind it level with its hips, tapering to a point, as long as its head is wide; the torn black bicycle seat is moved down to sit low and flat on the middle of its back like a saddle, just above the tail, its bent chrome stem poking out down and back under it, nothing of it up by the head; the huge round snout is a little bigger and rounder, hanging out well in front of the face below the eyes. Nothing else changes, no dirt, no texture.

4. Poses, three to a 16:9 image (two for the wall and death) with the design as the reference, cut apart at the
figures' own outlines, each at the scale it was drawn (`body` sizes every pose alike). The prompts are the poses in
tools/art3d/flat/kinds/dino.ts, after: "The same character exactly as in the reference image: ... in the same
three-quarter view facing right, with its long tail sticking straight out behind and the torn bicycle seat low on
its back. Draw it three times side by side in one row: three separate figures of the same size, with wide white
space between them, none touching or overlapping, each in its own pose. Left: ... Middle: ... Right: ... Nothing
else changes: no ground, no shadow on the ground, no motion lines, no text, no other figures."
Sheets: walk-1, walk-2, idle2 | run-1, run-2, air-1 | air-2, cheer, cheer2 | wall, death.

5. Hand edits: in air-1, run-2 and cheer2 the far pupil was drawn touching the snout's line and traced as part of
it, so the eye finder saw one eye: each is painted over in the eye's cream and drawn again a little up and back,
clear of the line.
