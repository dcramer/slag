How design.png and the pose drawings were made (2026-11-07). Every image was 2 credits (no free images left): 36 credits, 18 images. The drafts are in drafts/, the sheets in sheets/, the cut poses in flat/ (the wall slide mirrored: it was drawn facing left). No prompt was refused.

1. Design, three rounds of two (`node tools/art3d/style.ts design cleaner`).
   - Round 1, "a tall lean cartoon secret agent ... about four heads tall": two near twins, a realistic thin man about seven heads tall, the staplers hanging straight down beside his legs (not kept).
   - Round 2, "a huge head, a third of his whole height, on a slim body with long legs": drafts/design-2b.png the better, a quarter-height head. Its wear edit (2 drawn, not kept) swung the tie off the shirt and didn't move the staplers. Design reads (sil.py in the scratch folder: black over colour at 60 px, beside the boxer, raider, sneak, warrior, plumber and engineer designs, the suit recoloured by recolour.py): in its own slate suit "Agent 47 (Hitman), medium"; in orange "unsure: very thin, plain upright figure with a bald head and an orange jumpsuit", "Gordon Freeman (low)", "could be Agent 47 dressed as a prisoner"; green and pink "Agent 47, low". Mocks on it for 0 credits (the staplers swung out from the legs, a bigger sticker, a wider V, dark lapels, L-shaped pistols) moved orange no further: sized to the cast's height, a lean man is a stick and his props are specks.
   - Round 3, the brief in style.ts, "a chunky cartoon toy figure ... about two and a half heads tall": drafts/design-3b.png kept (the other drew the sticker on his forehead, a black tie and a drop shadow). Design reads: orange "Agent 47 (Hitman), medium: bald head, suit and a gun in each hand" and "Agent 47 (Hitman), low"; green "Agent 47 (Hitman), medium"; pink "Dr. Eggman (Sonic), low".

Orange read: "Agent 47 (Hitman), low: bald head with a mark on it".

3. Poses, three to a 16:9 image with design.png as the reference, each drawn twice, cut with `build.ts cut cleaner SHEET … --design`. Each prompt is the template below with three poses:
The same character exactly as in the reference image: the same design, proportions, colours, wear and tear, line and flat white background, in the same three-quarter view facing right. Draw him three times side by side in one row: three separate figures of the same size, with very wide white space between them, none touching or overlapping, each in its own pose, always the huge smooth bald head with the white barcode price sticker on the back of his skull, the broad square suit with the white shirt V and the crooked red tie, a big silver stapler held like a pistol in each black-gloved fist, low and out from his sides with white background round it, black shoes. Left: … Middle: … Right: … Nothing else changes: no ground, no shadow on the ground, no motion lines, no text, no other figures.
   - sheetA (two unhurried walking steps; standing, the near stapler raised and held out level at chest height): A-2 -> flat/walk-1, walk-2; A-1 -> flat/idle2.
   - sheetB (two upright runs; a jump with the shoes tucked): B-1 -> flat/run-1; B-2 -> flat/run-2, air-1.
   - sheetC (a fall with both arms flung out wide; straightening the tie at his collar; a smug little hop, eyes shut): C-1 -> flat/air-2, cheer; C-2 -> flat/cheer2.
   - sheetD, the far template ("The same character as in the reference image … drawn at the same size. Draw him twice side by side …", a wall slide and knocked out on his back with the staplers dropped beside him): D-1 -> flat/death; D-2 -> flat/wall (drawn facing left with a palm up on the wall, mirrored). Neither wall slide bent its knees.

4. The sticker (sticker.py, drawn/ -> flat/ and design.png): traced as drawn, its thin bars and grey peeled corner ran into a dark grey blot on the skull, a bruise or a third eye at play scale. Made one white label with three bold bars on every drawing where it sits inside the head (all but the death, where it hangs off).
