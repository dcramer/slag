0. Mock first, free: the old drawings edited by script (the dark hat erased and a huge pale flat hat drawn level, the hem flared, the waist pulled in, then long blade claws and a wide neckline), built and read through tools/art3d/blind-read.sh on the colour sheet. Pale hat alone: "unsure: tall woman in a long green gown with a wide brimmed hat"; "Lady Dimitrescu, low". With claws ~4 px: unsure; low. Claws ~6 px, fanned clear of the hat, and the neckline: "Lady Dimitrescu (Resident Evil Village), medium: very tall, long floor-length dress, wide-brimmed hat, long claws on one hand"; "Lady Dimitrescu — medium — very tall woman, floor-length gown, wide-brimmed hat, long claw-like fingers". The mock passed, so Tripo redrew it: drafts/mock.png.

1. Design, a clean redraw of the mock (--ref drafts/mock.png, 2 drawn, drafts/design-1..2, near identical; design-1 kept):
Redraw the character in the reference image as one clean hand-inked 2D cartoon game character, the same pose, proportions, colours and plain flat white background, the same three-quarter view facing right. Keep every shape as big as it is: a huge pale cream sun hat (#e4dccb) with a low flat crown and a black band, its round brim flat and level and far wider than her shoulders, the back end of the brim bent down where it was sat on; short black hair under it; very pale skin, red lips; a wide neckline showing pale collarbones with a string of big white pearls; the long plum gown (#7a2e4a) pulled in tight at the waist and flaring out wide to the floor, its hem torn; her raised front hand with four very long pointed silver blade claws (#b8b4c8) fanned out up and forward, each as long as her forearm, white background between each claw, one finger short and bare. Very tall and slim. Flat colour in two tones, a thin dark outline, no texture, no text.

2. Poses, three to a 16:9 image with drafts/design-1.png as the reference, each sheet drawn twice, cut with `build.ts cut vampire SHEET … --design`. The template, with the kind's KEEP (tools/art3d/flat/kinds/vampire.ts):
The same character exactly as in the reference image: the same design, proportions, colours, wear and tear, line and flat white background, in the same three-quarter view facing right. Draw her three times side by side in one row: three separate figures of the same size, with very wide white space between them, none touching or overlapping, each in its own pose, KEEP. Left: … Middle: … Right: … Nothing else changes: no ground, no shadow on the ground, no motion lines, no text, no other figures.
   - sheetA (inspecting her claws, two glides): A-1 -> walk-1, walk-2; A-2 -> idle2 (the claws lowered at her side: the model drew that instead of an inspection, and it is her own second idle).
   - sheetB (a stride hitching the gown, a run with the gown streaming, a ducked jump): B-2 -> run-1, run-2, air-1 (B-1's jump folded the claws into her body).
   - sheetC (falling, a cruel laugh, a curtsy): C-1 -> air-2; C-2 -> cheer2 (the laugh's claws were raised against the brim: replaced, step 3).
   - sheetD, the far template (the wall cling side on, knocked out on her back with the hat beside her): D-1 -> wall, death.

3. Blind read of that set, two fresh readers: "unsure – tall, thin green figure in a long gown with white spiky hair and claw hands"; "unsure — tall, thin figure in a long green dress with a tall pointed headpiece and a clawed hand". The claws raised beside the brim's end joined it into one spiky head, and the hat was smaller than the mock's. So:
   - The design again (--ref drafts/design-1.png, 2 drawn, drafts/design2-1..2; design2-1 kept): "The same character exactly as in the reference image: … Change only this, big and obvious: her pale cream sun hat is much bigger, its flat round brim half again as wide, reaching far out on both sides, still level, with the low flat crown and black band; her front hand is lowered to chest height and held out far in front of her, clear of her body and well below the brim, its four very long silver blade claws fanned out forward and slightly up, white background between each claw and between the claws and the brim. Nothing else changes, no texture." Its brim came back too wide for the box: `python3 bighat.py drafts/design2-1.png design.png 0.7 1.3` (the hat lifted out and pasted back 0.7 as wide and 1.3 as thick) is design.png.
   - The laugh (--ref design.png, 1:1, 2 drawn, drafts/cheer-1..2; cheer-1 kept as flat/cheer.png): "… A new pose: a cruel triumphant laugh, standing tall, chin up, mouth open laughing, the other hand on her hip, the clawed hand held out far in front of her at shoulder height, its four very long silver blade claws fanned up and forward like a spread fan, well clear in front of and below the brim …".
   - The hat on idle2, walk-1, walk-2, run-1 and run-2 drawn a quarter wider to match (`bighat.py flat/NAME.png flat/NAME.png 1.25 1.12`, from the cuts in step 2); air-1, air-2, wall, cheer2 and death keep their drawn hats (already big, or the edit marked the face).
