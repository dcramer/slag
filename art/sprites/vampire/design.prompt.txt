The same character exactly as in the reference image: the same pose, design, proportions, colours, line and flat white background. Change only this, and make each change big and obvious: the hat's wide brim is bent and droops down steeply on its back side where it was sat on, a big crease across it, the crown still low and flat; one of the five long silver nails is missing, that fingertip short and bare; the hem of the gown is torn into a big ragged notch at the front; there is a gap in the string of pearls where one is gone. Her face, her height and the raised hand stay as they are. Nothing else changes, no dirt, no texture.

The same character exactly as in the reference image: the same design, proportions, colours, wear and tear, line and flat white background, in the same three-quarter view facing right. Draw her three times side by side in one row: three separate figures of the same size, with very wide white space between them, none touching or overlapping, each in its own pose, KEEP. Left: … Middle: … Right: … Nothing else changes: no ground, no shadow on the ground, no motion lines, no text, no other figures.
   - sheetA (two glides, idle2 inspecting her nails): A-1 -> flat/walk-1, walk-2; A-2 -> flat/idle2 (A-1's was the design again).
   - sheetB (a stride hitching the gown, a run with the gown streaming, a ducked jump): B-2 -> flat/run-1, run-2, air-1. B-1 drew the runs facing left.
   - sheetC (falling, a cruel laugh, a curtsy): C-1 -> flat/air-2, cheer, cheer2 (C-1 kept the bent brim and her heels; C-2's fall was barefoot). Neither raised the hand higher for the laugh.
