How design.png and the pose drawings were made (2026-11-06). Every image was 2 credits (no free images left), no refusals. drafts/ holds the designs and edits, sheets/ the pose sheets, drawn/ each drawing as cut (and the design as edited), flat/ and design.png each made ready to trace by prep.py (the eye repainted apart by eyes.py). r1/ holds the first round (the plume ponytail), its sheets and its drawings as cut; its prompts are sections 1 to 3 below; r2/ holds the rope that replaced it (section 4), and section 5 is the fountain drawn now.

1. Design, round 1 (2 drawn, drafts/design-1, design-2; a prompt of its own, under 900 characters; the brief is BRIEFS.genie):
A single original cartoon character for a tiny 2D platformer sprite, full body, seen side on in profile facing right, on a plain flat white background, no ground, no text, no other figures. Flat bright colours, a thin dark hand-inked line, one hard cel shadow, no gradients, no fine detail. A slim dancer girl standing light on her bare toes. Her signature: a huge thick purple ponytail (#7a4ab8) as long as she is tall, springing up from the very top of her head and streaming straight out level behind her like a flag, white background under it all the way, tied at the crown with a big white shower-curtain ring. A crumpled tin-foil tiara on her brow, big curtain-ring hoop earrings, a short cropped top (#d4466c), bare midriff, a dark sash, huge puffy pink net-curtain harem trousers (#d4466c) gathered at the ankles, tan skin, a playful grin, dot eyes.
   design-1's ponytail flew level, design-2's hung in an S. Design read of design-1 black at 24 px (×3) among seven cast designs (the plumber, the raider, the elf, the fighter, the archer, the slayer, the hunter), two fresh readers: "Hair-whip half-genie (the Shantae parody) (medium): a thin, tiny body under a huge mass of hair that sweeps up and back in a long arc" and "unsure. Both paired her with the elf's cap ("long hair that streams back from a small body"). The body was too thin to be a body.
