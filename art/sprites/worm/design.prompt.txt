How design.png and the pose drawings were made (2026-11-04, round 8 2026-11-06). Eight design rounds; design.png and flat/ are round 8's, for the clean colour read. Every image was 2 credits (no free images left this month): rounds 1 to 6 took 60 images (120 credits), round 7 took 12 (24 credits), round 8 took 10 (20 credits). Each round's candidates are in r1/ to r4/, r6/ and r7/ with their sheets/. r1/flat to r4/flat and r6/flat hold those rounds' cut and prepped poses (round 5 redrew round 4's poses bigger).

After cutting, `python3 -I art/sprites/worm/prep.py` runs on the design and every pose. The cord trailing along the floor is taken out (kept, it traced as a dark loop on the floor that read as a tail), and with it any loose piece apart from the figure (round 7's dropped dryer in the knockout). The whites the head closes in (eyes, teeth) are repainted a pale lavender, so `holes` cuts only the background. A drawn wall line is whitened first: the sheet's `-nowall.png` keeps only the low-chroma darks of the columns that are dark most of the way down.
