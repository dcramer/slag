How design.png and the pose drawings were made (sixth round, 2026-11-04). Five rounds before it named him at low or low-medium at best: a narrow rounded blob on little legs, the can at the end of his arm "something blocky", a battery filling the notch behind. This round draws the outline the original is known by: a wide stance on huge flared boots, a small body, a ridge down the helmet with an ear disc standing proud, and the forearm itself the cannon. Every image was 2 credits (no free images left); the candidates are in r6/ and r6/sheets/. 15 images, 30 credits.

The same character exactly as in the reference image: the same wide stance with the legs far apart, the same huge flared bucket boots, the same three-quarter view facing right, line, flat colours and flat white background. 3. Wear (2 drawn from ears-1, the second kept as design.png: the dent landed in the helmet; the first's went into the ear disc), r6/worn-*.png:

Change only this, and make each change big and obvious: a big round dent is knocked into the back of the helmet, behind the ridge, its outline flattened there; the tin-can cannon is bound onto his forearm with a wide band of pale grey tape (#c9ccd2) wrapped round where the arm goes into the can; the can's muzzle rim is crumpled in on one side; the far boot has been repainted a lighter blue (#5f8fd8) and is a little bigger than the near one.
