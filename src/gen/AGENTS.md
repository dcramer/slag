# src/gen: procedural generators

Every generator is deterministic given its seed: same seed (and theme), same output, on every machine.

- `generateLevel` is a pure function of its arguments and of nothing generated before it: the browser builds the next levels ahead in a worker (`src/level-ahead.ts`) and the match takes them through `buildLevel` (`level/pregen.ts`). Module-level caches are fine only if they can't change an answer. A new way to change the lobby's level (a rule in `rulesProfile`, a menu action) adds its requests to `Match.upcoming()` (rules go in `LEVEL_RULES`); one that isn't built ahead freezes every peer while the main thread generates it.
- Reachability questions about one level go through one `ReachCache` (`src/sim/reach.ts`): it re-traces only what a change touches, with exactly a fresh analysis's answer. Pass the level's cache down instead of calling `analyzeReach` again.

- Randomness only from a seeded `Rng`; derive independent streams with `deriveSeed(seed, 'label')` so adding a roll in one place doesn't shift every other stream. No `Math.random`, wall clock, DOM, or imports from `render/`, `input/`, `audio/`, `ui/` (lint enforces this).
- Level pipeline (`level/index.ts`): pick theme → `generateTheme` look → profile (with the level's difficulty, `level/difficulty.ts`) → stage concept (`stage/`, `rollConcept`, fixed per level; about 20% plain) → the level's builder composing the theme's kit (`level/builders/`, `level/sets/`; see Set kits and level builders below; with a concept it calls `claimSite` before laying the ground and `raiseLandmark` after) retried until `level/validate.ts` accepts it → lanes (kept out of the landmark's air) → the landmark's pieces, every one required, on the terrain's own footing (a failure retries the attempt; 8 of those and the level goes plain, `GenInfo.conceptFallback`) → weather (rain or snow the landmark can't keep the solution in stays clear, so weather never changes a level's terrain) → filler set pieces from the concept's `family`. Falling back to the legacy sculptor or the flat floor counts as `fallback`, and tests assert it never happens. The whole sculpt is a draw: once a sculpt is proven, its precision (`level/precision.ts`: tight jumps on the more forgiving route) has to fit the difficulty's band, or the pipeline draws again, with a layout of its own (profile and concept from salted labels, the seed's gravity and difficulty kept), up to the difficulty's `draws`, then takes the nearest. Draw 0 rolls exactly as a level always did. A knob that should get harder with the level reads `difficultyKnobs()`, set beside the probe's gravity, rather than taking a new parameter.
- Stage concepts (`stage/<theme>.ts`, in `CONCEPTS`; docs/SETPIECES.md): the landmark's structure is tiles, so the static proof covers it, and movers are never on the proof: every concept keeps a static way past itself. The one exception is a `carrier`, whose pieces go in before the proof and whose crossings are proven by `level/ride.ts` (`rideProof`) and stored on `Level.rides`, which reach.ts walks. Its gap must be at least `carrierReach()`, beyond what parts close, and the level still needs its parts elsewhere.
- Solvability policy: almost no level is passable bare. Each asks for one part, or two on a theme's `TWO_PART_ASK` share of levels (tuned so each theme lands near `TWO_PARTS` needing two; a new theme starts at `TWO_PARTS` and gets an entry once `pnpm level --stats` shows its share), decided once per level by `rollChallenges`, and the pipeline rejects a bare-passable sculpt until `LAST_RESORT`. Every level is proven solvable with at most `MAX_BRIDGES` static `BRIDGE_W` strips, and `level.solution` is that count: `generateCrate` must always cover it, so it has to be the real minimum. Ask `src/sim/reach.ts`; never hardcode jump distances. Size hard crossings with `hardGap(rng, parts)` and jumpable ones with `jumpGap` where the ledge can be run on, `hopGap` from anything shorter; their `min` is in normal-gravity columns and stretches with the walking reach (`reachMin`). Keep landmarks off a hard crossing's banks (`keepClear`).
- Fresh levels are winnable (`level/winnable.ts`): a plan is at most `MAX_BRIDGES` bridges that reaches the flag while keeping clear of every part's barrier (`sim/props/barrier.ts`). Each round re-proves it to deal the crate; parts may seal a level (no placement is refused for it), and a round with no plan deals a demolition part that clears parts (`generateCrate`, `clearsParts`). A level starts from `level.solution`, so set pieces go in only if it still holds that way (`planHolds`, the placer's `keep`). Reach doesn't ride movers, so every set piece also passes `noFreeRide`: anything you can stand on, parked at 8 phases of its cycle as static ground, must not let a bare run reach the flag (`tests/stats/free-ride.test.ts`). Proven carrier rides (`level.rides`) are part of that baseline.
- Mostly open: `level/routes.ts` scores how much of the fastest route has no alternative lane (`MAX_SHARED`), with the solution's parts in place, on the graph `reach.ts` produces. `judge` reports it, the pipeline enforces it, and `level/lanes.ts` adds a second route to a sculpt that lacks one. Give it a head start: record hard crossings with `hintGap` so lanes stay off them, set `g.carve` in solid rock, and leave air above the ground route.
- Weather (`level/weather.ts`) is rolled after the terrain is final and can only be turned down, never reshape anything, so it can't move the parts shares. Anything new that touches the sim needs a gate there, proved on `analyzeReach`; its `airs` argument runs the ghost in a steady side wind.
- Decide "does this level get a challenge" with `rollChallenges`, which is fixed per level. A per-attempt roll is silently undone by retries, because harder shapes get rejected more.
- Set pieces (`setpieces/`): per-theme `Recipe` lists (`<theme>.ts`, in `SET_PIECE_RECIPES`) built from `builders.ts` and validated by simulation in `place.ts`. They are ordinary `PlacedProp`s with `owner = -1` and uids from `SET_PIECE_UID`, stored on `level.setPieces`, avoid protected rects, and must not cut the path.
- Looks (`theme/presets.ts`, `RECIPES`): palette, sky, skyline, terrain material, liquid, decor, grade per theme. A theme must read as itself at a glance.
- A theme changes how you move only through shared pieces, never a new physics path: its kit lays per-tile ground (`Shape.ground`, kinds in `sim/ground.ts`; glacier ice via `glaze`), and `THEME_GRAVITY` in `level/profile.ts` sets its gravity on Auto (space). reach.ts and every ghost walk on both, and the builders' gaps are measured at the level's gravity, so the solvability policy holds unchanged.
- Player prop archetypes (`props.ts`): `RECIPES` + `ARCHETYPE_WEIGHTS`. A recipe rolls a part's identity, then returns `sized`, which may only scale: no rolls in it, and no size words in names (`rollPart` adds them). `generateRoster` is the match's list of parts, sized by the Parts rule (`rosterSize`), from the seed alone and with a longer roster starting with the shorter one; `generateCrate` draws only from it, always with a bridge and a hazard.

- Decor (`decor/`): where the small scenery goes is decided here (`placeDecor`, from the level's seed), and `render/decor.ts` only draws it. Nothing in the sim knows decor exists.

Verify: `pnpm level <seed> --theme <t>` for the ASCII map, `pnpm level --stats 50` for fallbacks, timing and `needBridges`, and `pnpm shot levels --themes all` to look at the result. When you tune a share on purpose (parts needed, styles, set piece variety), run `pnpm test:stats` too; otherwise leave it to CI.

## Generators, contracts and the conformance suite

Every kind of generated thing has one registry, keyed by theme where it applies, and declares its contract as data on each entry. The engine enforces the contract twice: while generating (a violating instance is rejected and retried through the pipeline that already exists), and in `tests/conformance/`, which runs every registered entry of every kind over a fixed small set of seeds, sizes and gravities. Register something and it's under every check at once, with no new test to write. `kinds.ts` has the table and the per-theme minimums (5 landmarks, 12 decor pieces, 3 unique hazards; `tests/conformance/themes` lists what each theme still lacks as todos).

A conformance failure names the rule and the entry (`landmark/castle/haunted-wing/ways: seed 2 classic normal: 72% of the way past it has no second way`). Fix the entry. A known violation that can't be fixed in the change that found it goes in `WAIVERS` (`tests/conformance/lib.ts`) with the reason; never waive a new entry.

### Add a landmark (stage concept)

`stage/<theme>.ts`: `export const MILL = defineConcept({ ... })`, listed in the theme's array in `CONCEPTS` (`stage/index.ts`); the builder calls `claimSite` (or the kit's `site`) and `raiseLandmark` (see the pipeline above). A plain `Concept` literal still works: absent contract fields take the same defaults. Build it from `stage/kit.ts`: the crossing it owns (`bankKit` with the theme's bank and two-part share, `bankCrossing`, gaps from `hardGapRange`), ledges, passages, shells and the anchors the tests read (`ways`, `record`, `listSig`). `slag/no-copied-kit` (lint) stops a theme file redefining one; give the shared one a parameter instead.

| Field                                                 | Contract                                                                                                                     | Checked while generating                                                        | Checked in conformance                                                                                                         |
| ----------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `sizes`, `routes`, `fits`, `weight`                   | where it's rolled                                                                                                            | `rollConcept`                                                                   | it stands up on most of the levels it says it fits                                                                             |
| `scale` (`large` default, or `grand`)                 | large: a third of the view along its biggest side; grand: it owns the view (`LANDMARK_RULES`, `measureScale`)                | `landmarkFaults`                                                                | every level                                                                                                                    |
| `footing` (`grounded` default, `hanging`, `floating`) | every structural column stands on terrain, or a backdrop shell that does (`footingFaults`); floating only in `FLOATS` themes | `landmarkFaults`                                                                | every level, and the theme                                                                                                     |
| `ways` (default 2)                                    | at most `MAX_SHARED` of the way past its columns has no second way (`Routes.landmark`)                                       | `landmarkFaults`                                                                | every level                                                                                                                    |
| `carrier`                                             | static proof unless it's a proven carrier                                                                                    | the pipeline (`rideProof`, judge before pieces)                                 | `rides` exactly when a carrier; the solution needs no set piece                                                                |
| (its pieces)                                          | every one goes in, keeps the solution, gives no free ride, warns before it hurts, leaves a window                            | `placeLandmark`: `finalize`, `keep`, `noFreeRide`, `tellFaults`, `windowFaults` | the same, plus art for every module and `beamOf` art for what reaches through space                                            |
| (its box)                                             | out of the spawn and goal pockets                                                                                            | `landmarkFaults`, `claimSite`                                                   | every level                                                                                                                    |
| `varies`                                              | what its seed changes, in words; the engine measures it                                                                      |                                                                                 | 2+ structural parameters (`layoutOf`: box, tiles, pieces, footprints, placement) differ between any two seeds at the same size |
| `names`                                               | templates with `{place}`                                                                                                     |                                                                                 | the level is named after it                                                                                                    |
| `family`                                              | filler recipes of its own theme                                                                                              |                                                                                 | each is a recipe of the theme                                                                                                  |
| `codex`                                               | `about`: what it is and its ways through, a line or two for its page in the codex's maps                                     |                                                                                 | `tests/codex-maps`: it has one, and the codex's seeds raise it (`SHOT_SEEDS`, `src/codex/shots.ts`)                            |

The codex's maps (`src/codex/maps.ts`) show every registered concept under its theme with no codex edit: a still of a level raised round it, framed on its box, and the live race up close.

### Add a set piece or hazard

A `Recipe` in `setpieces/<theme>.ts` (`defineSetPiece` types one), in `SET_PIECE_RECIPES`. Build it from `builders.ts`. Flag it `unique: true` if no other theme hurts the same way: that's what the per-theme count of unique hazards reads, and conformance checks the flag against every other theme's set pieces and landmarks by mechanism (`hazardSignature`: what hurts, and the variant that changes how, like a gun's ammo or a swinger's head; looks don't count).

- While generating: `finalize` (swept cells free, nothing that hurts or moves near the spawn or goal, static solids don't cut the path), `keep` (the solution still holds clear of every cell with no `MIN_OPENING` window), `noFreeRide`, `tellFaults` (everything that switches on from nothing warns for at least `MIN_TELL` frames), and `windowFaults` (unless it never moves, every cell it hurts in is clear for more than `SAFE_WINDOW` frames between passes).
- Conformance: it can be placed; every module that hurts has a tell in `TELLS` (a new hurting module type needs an entry); its art exists, and reads `beamOf` if it reaches through space; it's never lethal every frame unless it never moves; no free ride; `unique` holds.
- The codex: a `unique` recipe shows up under its theme in the codex's maps by itself, caught at the busiest frame of its cycle. Give it `codex: { title, note, about }` (its name, a one-line joke, what it does); `tests/codex-maps` asks for it and for a picture at the codex's seeds.

### Add a decor piece

An entry in `RULES` in `decor/index.ts` (`defineDecor`): `place` (`top` grows on the ground, `under` hangs from a ceiling), `themes`, `weight`, `clear` (its room, at most `DECOR_MAX_CLEAR`), `span` for a run like a fence. Its kind becomes a `DecorKind`, so tsc asks for its art in `render/decor.ts` (`color`, `bake` and/or `live`). Every theme it lists draws from it from then on (`pickDecor`); a look with variants (space's station and asteroids) narrows its pool with `from`.

- While generating: `placeDecor` only puts a piece on a solid tile with its room open, off the spawn and the flag, and off a landmark that wears its own skin.
- Conformance: the same on real levels of every theme (`decorFaults`), its art exists, the level reaches exactly the same with decor placed (decor is cosmetic), placement and the look's pick vary with the seed, and a theme shows only decor registered for it, with at least one kind of its own.

### Add a part

A recipe in `RECIPES` (`props.ts`), keyed by a new `Archetype`, with its weight in `ARCHETYPE_WEIGHTS`, module art in `render/props/modules/` and a codex entry. Conformance: the same part from the same seed; `sized` only scales (same modules and motion, the same identity, the name changed only by its size word, never fewer cells as it grows); art and a codex entry for every module; no prop art draws a runner's eyes or mouth.

### Level shapes

Built by `BUILDERS` composing `SET_KITS` (next section). The pipeline enforces the policy (`judge`, lanes, `LAST_RESORT`, the fallbacks), and conformance checks each theme at classic/normal, wide/low and tall/high: no fallback, proven solvable with at most `MAX_BRIDGES` parts, mostly open, its own style, and the same level from the same seed. The shares (parts needed, two-part levels, open routes, styles, sizes, free rides, concept fallbacks) need many seeds and live in `tests/stats/`.

## Set kits and level builders

A level set (a theme) is a kit of pieces with declared attributes; a level builder, one per shape or mode, owns the plan and composes any set's kit, so every set builds every mode's level (owner direction, docs/DECISIONS.md → Levels). Builders never read the theme: what's set-specific comes through the kit (`tests/conformance/kits` checks the builders' sources for it).

- **What a set provides** (`SetKit`, `level/sets/<theme>.ts`, registered in `SET_KITS`; the kinds are `KIT_KINDS`):
  - **ground**: what a course stands on. Over ground (`CourseKit`): a surface profile along the builder's ramp, when the pockets take their rows (`early`, `max`, `near`), the step it relaxes to, and how it's laid. Over a drop (`ChainKit`): docks and each body's deck row. Cut from rock (`CarvedKit`): the rock to hollow.
  - **gap**: how a crossing is cut. A gap style declares its `pad`, `margin`, `range`, `bank`, `sample`, `sink`, `keepClear` and whether a two-part ask may be one crossing two bridges wide (`double`), plus its dressing (a collapsed bridge's stubs, a broken log). A chain lays out its voids; a carved set sizes its chasms.
  - **stamps**: what stands in it: bags by style on levelled ground (`stamps`, with widths, margin and how the ground levels under them) or on the ground as it lies (`standing`), a chain's bodies, a carved set's tunnels and timber.
  - **floor**, **connector**, **shell**: the tower pieces (`TallKit`: `deck`, `link` with `linkW` for a storey's rise, `margin` and `shell`).
  - **landmarks**, **set pieces**, **decor**: the registries above (`CONCEPTS`, `SET_PIECE_RECIPES`, `DECOR_RULES`).
- **What a builder may ask** (`BuilderDef`, `level/builders/index.ts`, in `BUILDERS`): the kinds it lists in `asks`, called through the hooks' signatures and nothing else. The builder owns everything that's policy: the ask (`rollChallenges` and what the landmark's own crossing takes), sizing every crossing (`hardGap`, `jumpCrossing`) and placing it, levelling stamps, the pockets, hints and undercuts, the landmark's site and `finish`. Today: `course`, `climb` and `descent` (one plan per kit shape, on the route's ramp: `builders/course.ts`, `chain.ts`, `carved.ts`) and `tower` (`sculptors/tall.ts`).
- **Adding a builder** (a mode's arena): a `BuilderDef` that `takes` its profiles and `asks` for kinds every set provides. Conformance builds it from every set's kit (`tests/conformance/kits`); a kind a set lacks shows as a todo there.
- **Adding a set**: its `SetKit` in `SET_KITS` with every kind, and tsc asks for the rest (see AGENTS.md → Theme).
- Hooks run in the builder's order and may only roll on `c.rng` where they always did: a refactor of a builder or kit is held to the parent commit with `pnpm tsx tools/sculpt-hash.ts` (identical hashes, identical levels), and a change meant to move levels with `pnpm level --stats 50`.
