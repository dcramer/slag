# src/brain: bots

A brain is an input device (docs/DESIGN.md → AI players): it fills a seat and produces that seat's `InputFrame`s, and nothing else crosses into the match. Only the machine that runs a bot runs its brain; every other peer just sees its inputs, so nothing here has to be deterministic across browsers. Built-in brains still take a seed and draw from `Rng`, so a headless trial replays exactly.

- **Senses** (`view.ts`, `types.ts`): `BrainView` is versioned (`BRAIN_VIEW_VERSION`) and rounded on purpose: positions in quarter tiles, speeds as words, parts as the cells they hold (`barrierOf`), never names or sim objects. A brain reads only the view. Bump the version when its shape changes.
- **Mind** (`brains/`, listed in `BRAINS` in `registry.ts`): `think(view)` returns an `Intent` (`ready`, `hover`, `pick`, `place`, `goTo`, `leap`, `giveUp`, `wait`). A new brain is a `BrainDef` there.
- **Motor** (`motor.ts`): turns intents into buttons. It may read the real match, as a player's hands act on the real game, but it only presses buttons: cursors move with the arrows and click, and a race plays the controller's own recorded moves (`movesFrom` in `src/sim/reach.ts`) toward the route's distance field (`route.ts`, which reads the race the way the solvability proof does, `fieldOf`). Moves are traced in the parts' winds as they will blow (`RaceMap.winds`). It drops a move that meets a hazard on the parts' cycles, a shot in flight, a part's solid moving into its way, or (in the air) a gust changing under it; looks one move past a landing a hazard is about to reach (with a walk-speed way onto each cell beside the quickest, for ice a run can't stop on); weighs a wait by the time it costs; and picks again whenever the body leaves its recording. Measure a change to it with Hard bots over every theme and size with parts placed (`tests/stats/bot-hard-routes.test.ts`, `botRace` in `tests/lib/bot-race.ts` takes `theme`, `size` and `parts`), and look at a miss frame by frame before fixing it: most are a position it walked into where every later move is hit.
- **Personality** (`personality.ts`): a bot's level (`Player.bot`, Easy/Normal/Hard, set on its card through the `bot` action) gives the ranges; its seed rolls where in them it sits. The motor reads the hands (reaction, slip, margin, hurry), the brain the pace. New behavior that should differ between bots goes in as a trait here, not as a branch on the level.
- **Seats** (`bots.ts`, `device.ts`): a bot is a local device of its machine (`bot1`, online `<peer>:bot1`), seated with a `join` action; `BotSeats.frames` is merged with the keyboards' before `net.tick`. Only the lobby's seated host (or an offline game) adds bots.

Nothing in `sim/`, `gen/` or `game/` may import from here (lint enforces it). Check a change with `pnpm vitest run tests/brain.test.ts`; when you change the motor, the route or a personality range, run `pnpm test:stats bot-finish bot-hard-routes` and compare the rates with the ones in those files.
